# The FOIA queue

The board carries 17 drafted Freedom of Information Act requests, each aimed at a specific agency and built out of the open questions already sitting on the nodes it covers, 117 node and question pairings in all. None of these requests has been filed. Stewart, 29 August 2026: 'The FOIA will have to happen way later but keep them there and ready.'

A question below is reproduced from the open questions recorded on the node the request names. Nothing here is a new question written for the export.
