# The eight threads

A thread is an authored walk through the board: a short chain of nodes where each step is joined to the next by a link somebody wrote down and sourced. These are the sequences the board considers its strongest, and they are the best place to start.

## The funded lineage: AAWSAP to the historical record

Thread name on the board: Lineage.

DIA records Senator Reid as co-sponsor with Senator Inouye of the July 2008 Supplemental Appropriation language directing DIA to investigate far-term foreign advanced aerospace threats, and that is the appropriation AAWSAP was run on. Sixteen years later AARO's own historical report is where the Department's account of that money, its contractor and its proposed successor sits.

**Step 1, Harry M. Reid** (year 2009, a person, board tier A). DIA records him as co-sponsor with Senator Daniel Inouye of the language in the July 2008 Supplemental Appropriation bill directing DIA to investigate foreign advanced aerospace weapon threats out to forty years in the future, a $10 million add.

**Step 2, Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP)** (year 2007, a program, board tier A). He is the reason the money existed: Reid co-sponsored the FY2008 supplemental $10M add with Senator Inouye, and that is the appropriation AAWSAP was run on.

**Step 3, Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP / AAITP)** (year 2009, a program, board tier A). AAWSAP is the official name and AATIP/AAITP is Reid's name for it; the Department has treated them as one contract and two labels, alternately.
