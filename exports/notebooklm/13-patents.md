## The findings on the filings

### Two patents lapsed

At Tier 1 documented. 37 CFR 1.362(b) exempts only plant and design patents. There is no provision for a federally assigned one, so both owed the first maintenance fee and neither was paid. The Navy prosecuted PAX 205 through a formal appeal to obtain the grant and then let it lapse.

Five weeks apart, same docket, same office of counsel. That weakens the reading that either was an isolated slip, and equally weakens the sharper one, since the sibling had no finding worth keeping. Both readings are on the node.
