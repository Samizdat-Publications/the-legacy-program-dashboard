# Board additions from the Michels corpus: a decision list for Stewart

**Written 2 September 2026, overnight, from 140 episodes of American Alchemy** (2.8 million
words of machine transcript), mapped by 35 agents, cross-checked against the 980 nodes,
triaged by six, and the top twenty candidates verified by four agents that opened the
records. The verification files `verified-michels-1.md` to `-4.md` carry the URLs, what each
document says, and a draft node in JSON for every row marked build. **Nothing has been
added to the board.** This is the list to say yes or no to, line by line.

`CLAUDE.md` section 2 governs. Every row below traces to a document that was opened; where
a record could not be opened from this machine it says NOT OPENED and no node is proposed.

## What the pass found in one paragraph

The transcripts are mostly testimony, and the verification pass kept turning the testimony
into something better: the documents the speakers were gesturing at. Twelve of the twenty
candidates survive as nodes that rest on a primary record. Four are the transcript's own
claims contradicted by the record they cite, which the board carries as findings. Four are
partly confirmed and should be built at the tier the record supports, not the tier the
speaker implied. One name (Matthew Sullivan) appears in no official record this session
could open and gets no node.

## F. What the tools learned

acronym, a nickname or a caption spelling. It now answers to a node's id, its
parenthetical acronym, its initials, given-name variants and one-edit spellings, each
shown in its self-test. NODE went 285 to 384 on the same input. `nro.gov`, `esd.whs.mil`,
`spaceforce.mil`, `nsa.gov` and `dodig.mil` refuse this machine; `sec.gov` refuses WebFetch
but answers curl with a contact user-agent; `image-ppubs.uspto.gov` and `vault.fbi.gov` work.
