## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — custody, funding, classification mechanism

Numbered for citation. All are **the speaker's claims**, several explicitly relayed
second-hand (Michels citing Grush/Grusch) rather than sourced directly.

**S1 — AAWSAP's real original purpose was a Lockheed material transfer, not paranormal
research.** Per Grush (relayed by Michels): Lockheed wanted to divest recovered material at
a specific facility; the "other government customer" was the CIA; the transfer was to be
made to AAWSAP so outside/civilian scientists could get eyes on it; the CIA blocked it in a
DIA/Lockheed dispute; the $21-22M went instead to Bigelow Aerospace facilities and equipment,
and the program's public-facing activity (Skinwalker Ranch, cattle mutilations, orbs)
became what it is remembered for. (01 / 0:52:49-0:54:26)

**S2 — Atomic secrecy chain as the UFO classification vehicle.** Per Grush, relayed by
Elizondo: Manhattan Project custody flowed into the Atomic Energy Commission, which flowed
into the Department of Energy "which has its own line of clearances" — described as a
parallel classification system layered on top of ordinary UFO secrecy. (01 / 1:13:12)

**S3 — Oppenheimer and Sarbacher as architects of 1954 AEC secrecy protocols.** Per Grush,
relayed by Michels: these two set up the original UFO secrecy protocols under the AEC, which
Michels reads via the Atomic Energy Act of 1954's flexible "special nuclear material"
definition (D8) as a mechanism that classifies any radioactive-adjacent recovered material at
nuclear-secret level regardless of whether it is a weapon. (02 / 0:33:16-0:34:07)

**S4 — A "cargo cult" national security posture that cannot talk to itself.** Michels's own
framing (not sourced to a named official): the US response to UAP is Balkanised across
aerospace fiefdoms (Lockheed, Northrop, BAE) precisely to frustrate FOIA, versus centralised
Russian/Chinese programs; tied to Karl/Carl Nell's Army Futures Command reverse-engineering
arms-race framing from "the debrief." (01 / 0:44:32)

**S5 — Physics classified as state secrets.** Per an NSC staffer (unnamed), relayed by Marc
Andreessen and Ben Horowitz to Elizondo: "we classified whole entire areas of physics ...
and made them state secrets," and that research "vanished." No document or date given. (01 /
1:16:44)

File 02's whole thesis: Robertson Panel -> Blue Book -> Condon Committee functioned as a

atomic-program ties (NDRC, Los Alamos, the McMahon Act), while — per Grush's separate claim

classification. (02 / 0:38:15-0:40:44)
