## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. DOC/HOST tags carried over from above.

**S1 — MJ-12's founding mandate (43, DOC).** Presidential Order, 24 September 1947; goals A–F
per the manual: recovery "by any and all means" of extraterrestrial material and entities;
establishing secure US facilities for study; establishing top-secret recovery teams; running
covert operations abroad jointly with the CIA; and maintaining "absolute top secrecy" over all
of it. (43 / 0:12:54–0:14:41)

**S2 — Recovery-site procedures and a press cover-story menu (43, DOC).** A preferred "total
press blackout," failing which a menu of cover stories (official denial; discrediting
witnesses via claims of hoax, hysteria or misinterpretation; deceptive statements naming
meteors, downed satellites, weather balloons, or toxic-spill contamination); detailed perimeter
and command-post procedures; witnesses "detained... until their stories have been evaluated."
(43 / 0:39:26–0:44:47)

**S3 — A custody hierarchy that supersedes ordinary rank or agency (43, DOC).** "OPNAC team
personnel will be given top priority at all times regardless of their apparent rank or status.
No person has the authority to interfere with the OpNAC team... by special direction of the
President of the United States." (43 / 1:02:04)

**S4 — Recovered-material properties, per the manual's own "preliminary analysis reports,
1947 to 1953" (43, DOC).** Material not matching any known terrestrial substance; described as
non-magnetic and showing "no... residual radiation"; possessing high strength-to-weight ratio;
some pieces engraved with undeciphered marks; mechanical components ("gears, etc.") whose
function or manufacture method could not be determined. (43 / 0:35:09–0:37:41)

**S5 — Ramsay's own stated personal conviction (43, HOST, explicitly his opinion, not the
document's claim).** He states he holds "very, very high" personal conviction that MJ-12, or
"at least the majestic program," exists, crediting this mainly to his regard for Bob Lazar as a
witness. (43 / 1:18:43–1:19:36)

**S6 — Blue Book's stated purpose versus its guest-described actual continuation (45, guest,
relaying his father).** Framed publicly and, per the guest, internally as a PR exercise to
dampen post-war "flying saucer" hysteria rather than a serious reverse-engineering effort; the
guest's father nonetheless continued informal, unstructured consulting on cases past Blue
Book's official close (Dec 1969/Jan 1970) — "no institutionalized involvement... more sort of
ad hoc," per the guest, who is explicit he does not know its extent. (45 / 0:11:32–0:12:30,
1:57:34–1:58:29)

**S7 — A "men in black" theory, the guest's own inference (45).** That his father's two known
encounters with unidentified men who silenced witnesses were with an Air Intelligence Service
squadron tasked with six-hour nationwide crash response and information containment; the guest
frames this, more broadly, as evidence the US government is not "one monolithic block" but
riven by "very intense turf battles" even within a single agency. (45 / 1:16:40–1:19:15)

**S8 — A single-witness "smoking gun" claim, explicitly unconfirmed by the guest (45,
relayed).** Danny Sheehan's claim, on a separate appearance, of having been shown into Blue
Book's records and seeing a photograph of a craft in snow; the guest says only that he
"believes what [Sheehan] says" but does not know what, if anything, his own father saw, and
is explicit that he cannot confirm it. (45 / 1:20:54–1:21:41)

**S9 — The testimony's own framing of its evidentiary weight (46, DOC via the Lorenzens'
editorial note, as read).** Presented as "probably the most controversial [testimony] ever to
come into our hands," with the authors' confidence resting explicitly on Dr. Fontes's
professional standing as a named co-author, not on independent physical corroboration; the
Lorenzens are described (by Ramsay, summarising, not quoted at length) as separately examining
