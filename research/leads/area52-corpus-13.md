## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered for citation. All are the speaker's claims, several explicitly stated as inference.

**S1 — Compartmentalization was real but the unit itself was small and tight.** Ford
describes a "pretty tight crew": Fred Atwater (operations), Bill Ray (boss), Ed Dames and
Jean Lesman (trainers), a secretary "Charlie Jeannie" and a civilian "Charlene"
[both UNRESOLVED, low-value staff names, not entered as entities]. (01 / 0:21:25)

**S2 — Sole-source intelligence, twice, on operations with lives or WMD-tracking at stake.**
The Higgins-location and Gaddafi-chemical-vessel cases are both described as acted upon with
no corroborating source — a submarine deployment based "on sole source, me." Ford frames this
as itself unusual and risky in the intelligence community's own terms. (01 / 1:01:03,
1:01:54)

**S3 — Records-handling after the 1995 transfer.** Ford's account: the CIA's outside review
had no clearances and never opened the transferred boxes (found still sealed a year+ later);
the 2017 CIA public release "shredded what was highly sensitive" and shuffled/scrambled the
rest before posting it online, and Ford's own reports were kept separate from other viewers'
by her superiors specifically to shield her from in-house criticism, meaning a chunk of the
program's output was never part of what got released at all. (01 / 0:52:47–0:56:07)

**S4 — The program's closure is attributed to belief/ideology, not results.** Ford: certain
"conservative" factions inside government treated remote viewing as religiously
objectionable ("work of the devil"), and this — not a documented performance failure — is her
explanation for the repeated attempts to defund it across ~20 years of operation. (01 /
0:49:53–0:51:43)

**S5 — Entertainment-industry cooperation with the Pentagon on UAP-adjacent content is overt,
not covert.** *Elio* (Disney/Pixar, 2025) credits DoD, Department of the Air Force and Space
Force cooperation in its end titles for a film centered on a child's desired alien abduction.
Speakers characterize this, as their own interpretation, as deliberate "soft disclosure"
messaging aimed at children — record the interpretation as the speakers' own, not as an
established fact about DoD intent. (03 / 1:04:24–1:12:37)

**S6 — A named nuclear-security witness claims direct interference with a warhead by an
object.** Guest (Kandil) describes **Mario Woods** [CONF as a person publicly discussed in
this circle; independent verification not attempted here] as a nuclear security officer who,
while guarding a missile at a site he calls **"November 5"** in, per the guest's own
uncertainty, "was it Montana?" [UNRESOLVED — location and site designator both stated with
doubt by the speaker], experienced missing time and watched a large spherical object
apparently begin to open the silo door and "activate" the weapon, prompting his own
contingency plan to physically block the door with his truck. **The primary account is
described as existing in full on Jesse Michels's channel, not in this episode** — this file
records only what Kandil says about it secondhand. (03 / 1:31:21–1:33:14)

**S7 — A researcher's compiled-testimony claim about nuclear-site personnel.** Kandil
attributes to **Robert Hastings** [CONF] — described as referenced for decades on the
UFOs-and-nuclear-weapons connection — the claim that self-reports from military nuclear-
installation personnel describing subsequent "close encounters of the third kind" are, in
Hastings's own estimation, undercounted relative to how many actually occurred, because
witnesses are said to hold back the more extreme parts of their accounts. This is Hastings's
claim as relayed by Kandil, not Kandil's own testimony. (03 / 1:44:32–1:45:26)

**S8 — Long-running private-funded research into the Aztec, NM 1948 crash claim.** Kandil
references authors **Scott and Suzanne Ramsey** [CONF] as having spent, per his account,
"over half a million dollars" of their own money across roughly 30 years investigating the
Aztec case, with **William Steinman** [CONF] named as an earlier researcher on the same
claim; a further documentary on the subject is described as forthcoming on Jesse Michels's
channel. No document, contract or dollar figure beyond the round "half a million" estimate is
given — record as a research-funding claim, not a sourced figure. (03 / 2:05:19–2:06:09)

**S9 — A claimed UK MOD visit to a UFO witness, unconfirmed by the MOD itself.** Per the read
article, two men carrying MOD identification visited witness "Philip Spencer" four days
after his story was shared with only four people (the article's author, Jenny Randles, Arthur
Tomlinson, Steve Ballom); the author's own letter of enquiry to the MOD went unanswered as of
the article's 1989 publication. Speakers speculate, as their own interpretation, that this
implies a tapped phone line, given a suspicious call from a purported (and denied) newspaper
the next morning. Record the speculation as speculation. (02 / 0:31:14–0:36:39)
