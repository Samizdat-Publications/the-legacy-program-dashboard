  custody thread the Gerb corpus carries is simply absent here.

  production interview. The one load-bearing thread pulled from it is Lazar's alleged hiring
  route through Teller and EG&G Special Projects (§1e), because that is the closest thing to

  Everything else — personnel names inside S4 beyond Mariani/Castilo, the craft's interior
  dimensions, lighting rigs, the reversed-flag account — is physical-reconstruction detail
  with no custody/oversight/funding content and is not recorded.

  "Eric Davis memo" the 4chan poster is asked about in episode 12 is almost certainly the same
  16 October 2002 Wilson–Davis meeting documented at length as D8 and S1–S9 in
  `research/leads/gerb-corpus-01-12.md`. Ramsay does not make this connection explicitly and
  clearly has not read the memo closely ("I'm not completely familiar with the Eric Davis
