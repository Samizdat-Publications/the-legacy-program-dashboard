unremarkable-looking FFRDC roles. (4 / 2:40:20)

still-classified KGB research department for "unknown flying and ... underwater objects," and
of standing Cold War-era deconfliction contacts between the US, UK and Soviet defense
ministries over unidentified underwater craft. Presented by Ilyumzhinov as personal knowledge
from his own Soviet Army service, not sourced further. (3 / 1:26:02–1:28:31)

Vatican Observatory. No document or date beyond "late 1980s" is given. (3 / 0:54:49–0:55:42)

**S6 — Immunity as the stated precondition for on-record testimony.** Eric Davis, in footage
Fox plays/paraphrases, states he would testify under oath before Congress about crash
retrievals, alien bodies, and reverse engineering if a presidential order absolved him of his
SCI non-disclosure agreements. Framed by Fox as the same ask he attributes to Grusch and
Elizondo. (4 / 2:33:36–2:35:15)

**S7 — Access tiering below the "full picture."** An unnamed source Fox will not identify by
name told him roughly 10 people "have the bigger picture" of the alleged programme and about
30 "know what you know" (the source's own level) — explicitly caveated by Fox as **not**
implying those 10–30 have the "full scope." (4 / 1:40:45–1:42:26)
