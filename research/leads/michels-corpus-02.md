## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, funding, custody, oversight, disclosure

**S1 — Reporting-line ambiguity as a deliberate obstruction (Shellenberger).** His own
sourcing changed the programme's described custody: first told it was a DOD programme, then
after publication told it is "actually managed by the Department of Defense but held at the
White House" — single-source, unconfirmed. He reads this as "somebody's playing some games
here to keep this stuff hard to track or easier to deny." (02 / 10;00-10;53)

**S2 — Debunking as a distinct information operation, not neutral skepticism
(Shellenberger).** Draws an explicit parallel between the 2020 Hunter Biden laptop

naming Graff's own later UFO-debunking book as "suspicious" given the personnel overlap; also
credits John Greenewald with showing the Pentagon's public position on UAP video releases
changed within days of itself. (02 / 18;33-20;25, 28;12-29;01)

**S3 — Nested compartments with non-correlated connections (Sheehan/Pines).** Consistent
with the Wilson-memo material already in the board: Pines's synthesis of Sheehan's decades
of reporting is that named institutional blobs ("the CIA," "Lockheed Martin") are a
simplification; actual control runs through "very individual... actors... aligned in very
complex overlapping relationships," with a "fractal structure of compartmentalization"
opaque even to people who think they hold power. (03 / 40;37-42;18)

inference, explicitly labelled speculative).** Carter's purge of senior CIA ranks did not

officers "ended up at... the Carlyle Group," citing Jacques Vallee's diaries repeatedly
naming the Carlyle Group in the same social circles. Also ties the McCandlish/Sorensen ARV

**S5 — Funding bifurcates into official-classified and "black" money once a programme
leaves official cover (Pines).** Two sources of funding for any covert operation: classified
public appropriations, and off-books money from criminal or investment activity generating
surplus. Pines explicitly flags Renaissance Technologies' return profile as the kind of
"black box money machine" this model would predict, while stating he has "no smoking gun."
