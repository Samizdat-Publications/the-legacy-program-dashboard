## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, funding, custody, oversight

All are the **named speaker's claims**, several explicitly flagged by the speakers themselves as inference or unsourced.

**S1 — Genetic-selection origin story.** Per Sherman's briefing officer: PPD began circa 1960 to genetically manage selected fetuses (demographically skewed toward poor families, on the theory that a predictable share would enter the military) so a subset would grow into "intuitive communicators." Sherman explicitly flags that he cannot be certain which specific details (the year, any reference to 1947-era contact) came from the officer versus his own retrospective interpretation. 14-4, 0:23:30–0:28:40.

**S2 — Onion-layer compartmentalisation, gray-inside-black.** The briefing officer described nested "layers," with an acknowledged black project (Sherman's overt Elint work and postings) providing the funding, manpower, and cover justification for an unacknowledged "gray project" (PPD) known to only a small subset of the black project's personnel. 14-4, 0:31:23–0:32:19.

**S3 — Recruitment and access protocol.** Contact only through captains; "pass-off" introductions confirmed by a physical signal (arms crossed, standing alone in front of a facility) rather than any verbal challenge; transport in an unmarked van with blacked-out windows on routes the recruit could not track; biometric (hand/retina) scanners at each access point; strict no-communication rule between two ICs sharing a facility. 14-4, 0:36:38–0:45:44.

**S4 — A second, corroborating vehicle traced to a named contractor.** Sherman describes twice seeing a second, unmarked white van near his training facility and later intentionally tailing it to Noise Cancellation Technologies Inc in Linthicum, MD — a company independently confirmed (per a cited 1991 Washington Post item) to have held a DARPA contract that same year. Sherman and Michels both frame this as circumstantial rather than proven. 14-4, 1:06:56–1:11:31.

**S5 — From training to operational use.** Sherman says that partway through his second posting, the content of what he was relaying shifted from what he understood as pure training/testing to material he interpreted as real-time abduction reporting (numeric "pain level" and "potentiality for recall" fields), at which point he says he felt "like an accessory" and began seeking to exit the program. 14-4, 2:39:07–2:44:36.

**S6 — Exit engineered around the program's own chain of command.** Sherman's PPD-side contact told him he likely could not separate from the Air Force even at the end of his enlistment. He instead disclosed his sexuality under the "don't ask, don't tell" policy through his *unclassified* chain of command — deliberately bypassing the PPD chain, which he believed would have suppressed the request. 14-4, 3:00:19–3:01:44.

**S7 — A FOIA exchange read as circumstantial confirmation.** The Air Force's redirection of an unrelated researcher's PPD-specific FOIA request to the NSA, and the NSA's subsequent invocation of Executive Order 13526 (a national-defense classification authority) rather than a flat "no such program" denial, is read by Michels as more suggestive of the program's existence than a standard non-response would be. This is interpretation, not confirmation. 14-4, 3:06:51–3:09:30.

**S8 — An alleged modern continuity and White House transfer.** The July 2025 *L'Espresso* article is the only source in this batch naming Project Preserve Destiny independently of Sherman's own account; it claims the program (or a program under that name) persists and that the Trump administration has moved certain SAPs, PPD reportedly among them, out of Pentagon oversight directly into White House control, describing a six-level secrecy structure matching what Sherman independently describes. No named source is given inside the article as quoted on air. 14-4, 3:16:31–3:20:13.

**S9 — CIA behavioural-research continuity via a corporate cutout.** Per John Marks's book: after MKUltra's official 1960s wind-down, the CIA's Office of Research and Development (under Stephen Aldrich) continued closely related behavioural-control research through a CIA proprietary (Scientific Engineering Institute), which was later folded into the private sector as a G.D. Searle subsidiary under Donald Rumsfeld's chairmanship, and ultimately sold into Monsanto. This is the clearest sourced contractor-lineage claim outside file 14-4. 14-3, 0:13:05–0:15:51.

**S10 — An unsourced facility-custody claim.** Michels's own assertion, explicitly given without a named source, that Bechtel (as a major secure-facility builder) would likely be "read into" any need for a secure facility for medical experiments "or perhaps even keeping alien bodies," and that Monsanto "may have historically been involved" with biological specimens. Record as speculation only; do not treat as sourced. 14-3, 0:15:51–0:16:00.

**S11 — A witness account of a contractor training pilots on recovered/reverse-engineered craft.** Lyn Buchanan's account (relayed secondhand by Jason Jorjani, not independently corroborated in this batch) of a Lockheed underground California facility housing an FAA-style reconstruction of an airliner/UFO collision, where he says he was asked to remote-view the craft's guidance system to help train pilots; Buchanan reportedly emphasised the disorienting "time displacement" problem as the central training obstacle. 14-2, 1:29:36–1:32:07.
