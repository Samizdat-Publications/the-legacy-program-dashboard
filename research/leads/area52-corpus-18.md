## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered for citation. All are **the speaker's claims**, several explicitly flagged by the
speaker themselves as guesses or inference.

**S1 — A private contractor, not a uniformed unit, ran Lazar's claimed hiring and site access.**
EG&G Special Projects hired Lazar via a referral from Edward Teller; the interview process was
split into a technical round and a separate personal/lifestyle round Lazar found "really,
really unusual." (02 / 0:15:28–0:17:03)

**S2 — Compartmentalisation by the "buddy system."** Lazar: "We all worked on the buddy
system... You can exchange information [or] ideas with that one person and that's it. There's
no talking to anybody else." (02 / 0:36:53)

**S3 — Deliberately seeded disinformation inside briefing documents, to trace leaks.** Barry's
explanation to Lazar, relayed by Lazar: "they put a bunch of crap in there. That way, if
information is spread around and the garbage they give different people is different... they
know exactly who to trace it back to." (02 / 0:51:38)

**S4 — Gravity manipulation research split three ways: propulsion, weapons, time.** Lazar
frames Project Galileo (propulsion), Project Sidekick (weapons) and Project Looking Glass
(time dilation) as "the three potentialities... in Space with the craft, propulsion, time with
time dilation and then weaponization." (02 / 0:44:22–0:45:11)

**S5 — Element 115 as the reactor's power source.** Lazar: "the reactor itself appeared to be
powered by a super heavy element, element 115... what we had was a stable version of it,"
worked by what looked like "a little accelerator, like a little cyclotron." He states he is
"increasingly convinced" the effect is not gravity itself but "another force of nature." (02 /
1:36:39–1:39:12)

**S6 — Cross-case craft-design consistency, offered as corroboration.** Lazar and Ramsay
compare the S4 "sport model" to Travis Walton's described craft (arched underside features)
and to Billy Meier's 1970s photographs, judging the resemblance close enough to be
"incredible" and "95%" alike; Ramsay separately raises a claimed Aztec-case witness at
Wright-Patterson who saw a craft become transparent under high voltage, which Lazar says
matches his own observation of the S4 craft's translucency. (02 / 1:26:13–1:28:50,
1:47:30–1:55:38)

**S7 — Underwater craft claims relayed secondhand.** Ramsay, citing an earlier appearance by
Lue Elizondo on this podcast: alleged 4K footage of a city-block-sized round object moving at
roughly 500 knots underwater, apparently satellite-sourced; and a separate claim attributed to
Tim Gallaudet of large craft moving at 400–500 knots underwater. Neither claim is sourced to a
named document, agency or date in this transcript. (02 / 1:44:17–1:46:39)

**S8 — A named claimant alleges a human-trafficking operation using "reverse engineered
craft."** Per an audience question read by Ramsay, describing Michael Herrera's account:
during a humanitarian mission, Herrera's unit allegedly encountered unbadged, unmarked
military-appearing personnel loading people onto an octagonal black disc; other witnesses he
later tried to contact declined to corroborate. **This is secondhand within this transcript —
Herrera does not testify directly in this file — and no unit, agency, country or date is
independently confirmed.** (04 / 2:47:35–2:49:26)

**S9 — "Humans as containers" claim attributed to Bob Lazar's S4 briefing papers, secondhand.**
Nanstiel, prompting Ramsay: "do you remember what Bob Lazar said when he read these briefing
papers at S4?... the military reported that the gray is considered humans as containers. And
we were given religion so that we wouldn't damage the containers." **This claim does not
appear in Lazar's own words anywhere in file 02 of this batch; it is Nanstiel's secondhand
characterisation only, and should not be merged with file 02's material as if Lazar said it on
this show.** (04 / 1:35:21–1:36:11)
