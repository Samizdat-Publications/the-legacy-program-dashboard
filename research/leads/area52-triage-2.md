**Written 2 September 2026** against `research/leads/area52-corpus-06.md` to `-10.md` and
`research/leads/area52-absent/group-2.tsv` (103 names). **Nothing here is board content and
nothing here is asserted as true.** Every row says what a named speaker claims, where the
claim sits in a lead map, and what record would have to be opened to justify a node. No
document was opened for this pass. `CLAUDE.md` section 2 governs.

Spencer book read aloud). 07 = ep. 40 to 42 (John Ramirez, ex-CIA, is file 04 and carries
almost all of the batch's structure). 08 = ep. 43 to 46 (the MJ-12 Special Operations Manual

file 09-3; the Joe Simonton case file is 09-4). 10 = ep. 51 to 54 (Joe McMoneagle is file 1).

DOC/HOST/guest split was re-read before ranking. Rows sourced to Chris Ramsay's asides rather
than to a guest are marked HOST. Batch 06 files 02 to 04, batch 08 files 43 and 46, and batch
10 file 4 are read-aloud episodes and are handled in section D.

**Counts.** A: 15 ranked rows, plus 1 named below the cut and 5 names attached to row 4.
