Two scope facts that shape everything below. First, five of the ten maps are thin because
their episodes were off-subject, and the mappers said so in their own section 4: batch 22
returns zero UAP hits on three of four files, batch 25 finds one structural item in four

witnessing, which is why so many of their names below resolve to a findable technical
record rather than to a testimonial claim. That is a feature: the strongest rows in this
