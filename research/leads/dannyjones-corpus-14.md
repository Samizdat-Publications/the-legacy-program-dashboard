## 6. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, custody, oversight (Kruse, file 2)

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are **Kruse's own claims**, stated as fact but with
**no document, case number or citation given for any of them** — this absence is itself the
structural feature of the episode.

**S1 — MKUltra formed from two merged threads.** Robert Becker's bioelectric/DC-current work,
plus a Nazi-linked doctor Kruse names "Kurt Plotkin" [PHON? — possibly a garbled reference to
Kurt Plötner, a documented Nazi-era mescaline researcher; treat as a search string], studying
mind control via peyote. (0:36:25)

**S2 — DARPA formed 1958 and absorbed the CIA/FBI's MKUltra-era findings**, then directed José
Delgado's remote bull-control experiments in Mexico as the next step toward a wireless,
light-based control mechanism. (0:37:17–0:40:49)

**S3 — The SV40 "fix" became the JFK motive.** Per Kruse, once Sarah Stewart/Mary Sherman's
work showed SV40 could not be neutralised (irradiation made it "more lethal"), the same
apparatus that tested it on a death-row prisoner pivoted to using it as a bioweapon against
Castro, and JFK's assassination is presented as the fallback when Castro could not be removed
via the tainted cigar plan. (1:13:35)

**S4 — General Groves as the origin node.** Per Kruse, Groves parlayed the Manhattan Project
into engineering the 1947 OSS-to-CIA transition and built "a militia outside the military,"
funded by unnamed Texas/Illinois/California companies, that persisted through the Eisenhower
and Kennedy administrations regardless of who held the Joint Chiefs. (1:48:37–1:49:51)

**S5 — Money is the throughline.** Kruse explicitly frames Social Security, Iran-Contra,
Roth/SEP IRAs, and COVID-era spending as sequential mechanisms for the same unnamed apparatus
to generate a large, unaudited pool of capital to fund covert operations. (1:36:26–1:39:48)
