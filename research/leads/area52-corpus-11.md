# AREA52 - DEBRIEFED transcript corpus — structural extraction, batch 11

**Source:** `dist/transcripts/md/`, the four files listed under `## 11` in
`research/leads/area52-batches.txt`.
**Extracted:** 2 September 2026.
**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** Every line records *what a named
speaker says*, attributed, with a file + timestamp pointer. Nothing here is asserted as true
and nothing here is graded. No quotation exceeds a short phrase; where the wording matters
the pointer is given instead so the claim can be re-read at source.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper) with proper-noun corruption. Names below are marked:

- **[CONF]** — spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure, organisation,
  document or programme I already know exists.
- **[PHON?]** — phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw caption
  form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — I could not reconstruct it with confidence. Recorded raw.

Timestamps are the marker nearest the statement, format `H:MM:SS`, matching the transcripts'
own `**[H:MM:SS]**` markers.

**Attribution note.** These transcripts carry no speaker labels. AREA52 - DEBRIEFED is hosted
by **Chris Ramsay**; three of the four files are one-on-one interviews (Ramsay + a named
guest), and the fourth is Ramsay solo. Where a passage is clearly the guest's own testimony
(first person, "I saw", "I was told") it is attributed to the guest. Where Ramsay is
narrating, summarising, or adding his own gloss — including hedged asides like "I think" —
it is attributed to Ramsay, and marked as his own uncertain claim where he hedges it himself.

## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

This batch carries almost none of the custody/oversight/funding material the brief asks the
board to track — no SAPs, no named contractors, no classification instruments. The two items
that come closest:

**S1 — Blue Book's single-witness intake policy as an explanation for non-investigation.**
Per Gary Wilcox's psychiatric study (Ramsay reading it verbatim): Project Blue Book's own
guidelines meant it rarely pursued single-witness reports without physical evidence, offered
as the likely reason Blue Book investigated the same-day Zamora case (multiple forms of
physical evidence, a police officer witness) but never took up Wilcox's case. (4 / 0:51:23)

**S2 — A witness's report reaching an official file only through a private citizen's
initiative.** The Wilcox case reached Tioga County's sheriff only because Priscilla Baldwin,
a private citizen with no official standing, independently contacted the sheriff's office
after hearing the story secondhand; her own notes were then incorporated into the official
investigation file. (4 / 0:04:41, 0:09:44)
