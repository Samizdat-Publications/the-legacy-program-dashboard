**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** These are not board content and
nothing here is asserted as true. Every row records *what a named speaker claims* (or, for
episode 20, what a named 1987 document claims, being read aloud on air), attributed, with a
file + timestamp pointer, so a later pass can check it. No quotation exceeds a short phrase;
where wording matters the pointer is given instead.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper), uncorrected, and corrupt names silently and plausibly. Names below are
marked:

- **[CONF]** — spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure, organisation,
  document or programme I already know exists.
- **[PHON?]** — phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw caption
  form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — I could not reconstruct it with confidence, or the name reads clean but
  is not a figure I can independently confirm. Recorded raw either way.

Timestamps are the caption marker nearest the statement, format `H:MM:SS`, matching the
`**[H:MM:SS]**` markers in the source files.

**Attribution note, this batch.** Three of the four episodes (17, 18, 19) have Chris Ramsay
(host) alone, or Ramsay with a single guest, and carry effectively no structural content — see
the file key and §5. The fourth (episode 20) has no live guest either: Ramsay reads a named
1987 document aloud, end to end, interleaving his own commentary. Every entity/document/claim
below from episode 20 is tagged **DOC** (John Lear's 1987 Paranet statement, as read on air)
or **HOST** (Ramsay's own aside, comparison or speculation) so the two are never conflated.
Nothing here is Lear's live testimony — it is a decades-old circulated ufology document,
already well known and already treated skeptically by ufologists generally; recording it is
not endorsing it.

## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS IN THE 1987 DOCUMENT (episode 20)

Numbered so the board can cite them. **All DOC — these are the document's claims, read
aloud by Ramsay, not asserted by this extraction as true.**

**S1 — MJ-12 as a self-perpetuating, non-governmental-in-practice body.** Established by
Truman in 1947 with twelve members; membership renews itself by appointment as members die;
document claims it still existed, expanded, in 1987. (20 / 0:12:27, 0:13:20)

**S2 — The 1969–71 "deal."** In exchange for technology, the US government (via MJ-12)
agreed to ignore ongoing abductions and suppress cattle-mutilation reporting; EBEs described
abductions as benign "monitoring." (20 / 0:23:59–0:24:52)

**S3 — Corporate/technical custody, not full government control.** Groom Lake was closed
roughly 1972–74 while an underground facility was built with EBE assistance; the document
states the resulting technology "could only be operated by the EBEs themselves," so it could
not be turned against them even if needed. (20 / 0:37:10–0:38:09)

**S4 — A reporting mechanism to the NSC.** Abductee lists were to be submitted periodically
to MJ-12 and the National Security Council under the deal's terms. (20 / 0:30:39–0:31:33)

**S5 — A jointly-run recovery/exploitation site with lethal force used against US personnel.**
Dulce is described as CIA-alien jointly occupied; a 1979 incident allegedly cost 66 soldiers'
lives in a failed rescue attempt, sourced only to "one source." (20 / 0:34:15–0:40:00)

**S6 — Discovery that the deal's scope had been exceeded.** Between 1979–83, MJ-12 allegedly
learned that abductions and tissue-harvesting (including of missing children) vastly exceeded
what had been disclosed to it, prompting a public-conditioning campaign (the document frames
*Close Encounters* and *E.T.* as products of this campaign). (20 / 0:39:02–0:41:39)

**S7 — A 1984 factional split inside MJ-12, resolved toward continued concealment.** One
faction wanted to confess; the majority chose continued secrecy plus development, under cover
of the Strategic Defense Initiative, of a weapon usable against the EBEs. (20 /
0:41:39–0:43:20)

**S8 — Two named code-word programmes, corroborated (per the document) by a real Senate
letter.** Project Aquarius (NSA) and, nested within it, Project Snowbird (flight-testing a
recovered craft at Groom Lake), the former's existence allegedly confirmed in a letter from
NSA's Director of Policy to Sen. John Glenn. (20 / 0:47:26–0:48:14)

**S9 — An intermediary of uncertain loyalty.** William Moore is presented by the document as
possibly working for MJ-12, feeding partial/managed disclosure; his associate Lee Graham was
investigated by DIS over documents traced to Moore, while Moore himself reportedly was not.
(20 / 0:48:14–0:50:38)

**S10 — Segregated captive-alien custody.** EBE1/2/3 held at a Los Alamos facility the
document designates "YY2"; Edwards AFB named as the only comparably secure alternate site.
(20 / 0:44:09)

**S11 — A religious-engineering claim, explicitly the document's most extreme assertion.**
EBEs allegedly claim to have created Christ and other biblical figures as genetically
engineered "containers." Ramsay (HOST) separately connects this to unrelated Bob
Lazar/S4 "blue documents" material — his own comparison, not in the 1987 document. (20 /
0:44:09–0:46:37)
