**Scope:** `research/leads/area52-corpus-24.md` through `-27.md` (the last sixteen episodes of
the DEBRIEFED catalogue, transcribed after the others) and
`research/leads/area52-absent/group-6.tsv`, 41 ABSENT names.

**Nothing here is asserted as true and nothing here has been added to the board.** Every row
records what a named speaker says on a podcast, plus the record that would have to be opened
before any of it could become a claim. `CLAUDE.md` section 2 governs: where a document,
statute, archive or series appears below it is an identifier to verify, not a citation this
pass has opened. Where I supplied the record rather than the speaker, the row says so.

and labels and by counting raw string occurrences. Every node id named in section C was
confirmed against that file. Section A tables of `area52-triage-1.md` through `-5.md` were
read; two ABSENT names in this group were already ranked there and are marked, not re-argued.

**Pointer format.** `map / file / TS`, where *map* is the batch file (`area52-corpus-NN.md`)
and *file* is that map's own episode number. Batch 24 numbers its files 1 to 4; batches 25 to
27 number theirs by episode (05 to 16).

**Counts.** A: 12 rows covering 14 ABSENT names. B: 10 rows covering 10 ABSENT names, plus 7
`[PHON?]` resolutions carried in under the brief's rule. C: 14 rows, seven of which are false
ABSENTs. D: 11 names in seven groups, plus the read-aloud exclusions.

**What this corpus is.** Sixteen episodes, one host (Chris Ramsay), and only five of them
carry a guest. Seven are the host alone reading a document or a forum thread aloud and
reacting to it. The yield is lopsided: two files carry almost all the structural content in
the group (batch 26 file 12, the NRO patch material, and batch 27 file 02, the Immaculate

posts.

**The strongest thing in this group is not a name.** Batch 27 file 02 is a near-complete
public read-through of a document the board already holds as a node, and it names five
contents the node does not record. That is section C row 1, and it is worth more than
