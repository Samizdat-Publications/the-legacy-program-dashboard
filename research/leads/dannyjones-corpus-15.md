# Danny Jones Podcast transcript corpus -- structural extraction, batch 15

**Source:** `dist/transcripts/md/`, batch 15 of `research/leads/dannyjones-batches.txt`, four episodes.
**Extracted:** 3 September 2026.
**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** This is not board content and
nothing here is asserted as true. Every row records *what a named speaker claims*,
attributed, with a file + timestamp pointer, so a later pass can check it. No quotation
exceeds a short phrase; where wording matters the pointer is given instead.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper), uncorrected, and corrupt names silently and plausibly. Names below are
marked:

- **[CONF]** -- spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure,
  organisation, document or programme I already know exists.
- **[PHON?]** -- phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw
  caption form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** -- I could not reconstruct it with confidence. Recorded raw either way.

Timestamps are the `**[H:MM:SS]**` marker nearest the statement, matching the source files.

**Attribution note, this batch.** All four episodes are Danny Jones (host) with a single
guest, plus an off-mic producer, "Steve," who pulls up images/articles/searches on request
and is not a speaker of substance (consistent with the convention used in batch 09).
Episode 271 is Danny Jones with guest Danny Goler (a DMT/laser researcher, not a defense or
intelligence figure). Episode 274 is Danny Jones with guest Adam Wyman (the "Dark Science"
YouTube channel). Episode 275 is Danny Jones with guest Greg Braden (author/former Cold War
defense-industry computer systems designer). Episode 276 is Danny Jones with returning guest
Matthew Cox (a former fraudster turned true-crime YouTuber). Where a passage is a joint riff
with no clear single speaker, it is marked "speaker unclear."
