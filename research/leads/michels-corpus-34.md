## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS -- compartmentalisation, funding, custody, oversight

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the **speaker's claims**, several explicitly
their own inference.

**S1 -- Manhattan Project secrecy is the direct architectural template.** Grusch: Oppenheimer
"created the classification that included the UFO stuff"; the guys involved in the Manhattan
Project "were overlaying the same ecosystem of secrecy" later used to protect UFO material.
He also floats, without sourcing, that an internal UFO-reporting practice inside the
Manhattan Project itself pre-dated and modelled Blue Book. (02 / 0:22:52, 1:21:13, 0:23:43)

**S2 -- Nuclear-material classification as the legal mechanism.** The 1954 Atomic Energy
Act's "special nuclear material" definition (anything emitting atomic radiation) is, per
Grusch, the statutory hook used to classify retrieved crash material under the same secrecy
regime as nuclear weapons data. (02 / 0:22:04)

**S3 -- Wright-Patterson as the need-to-know custody hub.** Grusch's Curtis LeMay/sitting-
senator anecdote: LeMay refused to discuss "the room" at Wright-Patterson even to a sitting
senator, becoming visibly angry. Only someone who had independently held CIA-Director-level
access (George H.W. Bush, later as President) is presented as plausibly briefed. (02 /
0:25:20--0:26:56)

**S4 -- Nested compartmentalisation limits even cleared insiders.** Grusch: "you compartment
it so much, and some people don't know really what they're working on. Only the people that
have the top-level accesses can look down on all facets of the pyramid." (02 / 0:27:50)

**S5 -- Pre-publication review as a disclosure choke point.** The DOPSR process requires a
redacting office to self-identify and cite a specific classification guide before removing
content from a cleared officer's public statement, which can then be legally challenged --
Grusch calls this "catch-22" for the reviewing office either way. (02 / 0:18:39--0:19:31)

**S6 -- Biologics distinct from the artifact/technology side.** Program-cleared sources told
Grusch that recovered biologics were assessed as non-human; he is careful to note he does not
know their job function or condition (alive/dead). (02 / 0:16:52--0:17:45)

**S7 -- A parallel CIA research thread links UFO contactees and Havana Syndrome patients**
through a shared neurological marker (caudate nucleus/putamen density), per Gary Nolan's own
account of being tasked by the CIA. (01 / 0:06:04; 02 / 0:38:48)

**S8 -- An "Invisible College" of insiders exists outside formal government channels.**
Nolan says he was introduced, via the CIA tasking, to a named group -- Jacques Vallee, Hal
Puthoff, Eric Davis, Robert Bigelow, Colm Kelleher [PHON? caption `Com Kelleher`] -- most of
