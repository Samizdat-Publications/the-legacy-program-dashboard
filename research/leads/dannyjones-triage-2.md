# Danny Jones corpus, triage group 2 (batches 06 to 10)

**Nothing here is board content and nothing here is asserted as true.** Every row records
what a named speaker says in a transcript, with the lead map and timestamp it came from, and
says what record would have to be opened before any of it could become a node. No documents
were opened in this pass.

**Scope.** Lead maps `research/leads/dannyjones-corpus-06.md` through `-10.md`, twenty
episodes. The ABSENT list is `research/leads/dannyjones-absent/group-2.tsv`, 106 names, all
of which are accounted for below: 24 in section A, 22 in section B, 3 in section C, 57 in
section D. Section B carries further rows that are NOT on the ABSENT list, marked as such,
because the brief asks for them.

**Citation form.** `06 f01 1:26:41` means lead map `dannyjones-corpus-06.md`, its file 01, at
that timestamp. Maps 07, 09 and 10 number their files by episode number, so `07 f398` is
episode 398 in map 07.

**Yield, by map.** 06 and 09 are the strongest: 06 is a full-length Robert Salas interview
plus the Daily Mail missing-scientists article the House Oversight letters footnote, and 09
sets TD Barnes on Area 51 directly against Annie Jacobsen on Alfred O'Donnell. 07 is dense
but almost entirely testimonial. 08 is one guest's own personnel-network hypothesis. 10 is
thin, but its patent-prosecution passage is the single most openable record in the group.
Eight of the twenty episodes contribute nothing at all and the mappers said so.
