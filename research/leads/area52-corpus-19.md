## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the speaker's own claims, several explicitly
labelled by the speaker as inference, hunch or hedge — recorded as such.

**S1 — A four-tier pressure taxonomy for witness handling, Corbell's own framework.** Asked
directly what happens to whistleblowers, Corbell lays out four escalating categories he says
he personally uses to describe what he has seen: **inert** (deniable, procedural pressure —
e.g. a superior "flexing" a chain of command all the way to the Secretary of War to compel an
interview that has already effectively been declined); **covert** (using an unstable
third party with pre-existing intelligence-community ties and personal animus toward the
target, "poked and prodded" into acting without being told to, so that no order is ever
traceable); **overt** (a direct call — "we are going to fucking kill you" — or a staged,
unmistakable confrontation); and a blended overt/covert form (a stranger physically marking
or "tagging" a target, meaning becomes clear only later). He is explicit that "vanishing" a
person, rather than a visible killing, is preferred specifically because a visible murder
"would make a lot of questions." (89 / 0:23:19–0:41:47)

**S2 — Alleged specific AARO tradecraft against a Navy witness.** Corbell's account: after a
witness (an active-duty senior/chief petty officer who had already testified publicly) declined
a follow-up interview, AARO staff allegedly called pretending to already know who else was
present when relevant footage was recorded (a fact Corbell says he had already made fully
public), in order to manufacture leverage; when declined again, a superior was then contacted
and told escalation would reach "the Secretary of War." Corbell frames the goal as not
punishing the individual but controlling/shaping "the emerging UAP narrative" more broadly, one
naval officer at a time. (89 / 0:27:35–0:30:06)

**S3 — Formal rule Corbell says he enforces on his own witness-vetting process, and its
breach.** Corbell says his condition for helping arrange any congressional UAP hearing witness
