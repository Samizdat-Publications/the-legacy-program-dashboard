## 1. The Hippler letter: RECORD, three nodes

Opened: **https://www.nicap.org/docs/HipplerLetters.pdf**, a five-page scan in NICAP's
document archive, fetched on 2 September 2026. The scan's own text layer is a poor OCR, so
the pages quoted below were read as page images rather than as extracted text, and every
quotation here was transcribed from the image of the page.

**What the file contains.**

| page | document |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Department of the Air Force, Headquarters United States Air Force, Washington D.C. 20330, **16 January 1967**, to Dr. Edward U. Condon, University of Colorado, Boulder, Colorado 80302. Signed "ROBERT R. HIPPLER, Lt Colonel, USAF, Science Division, Directorate of Science & Technology". Stamped "JAN 2 3 REC'D" and numbered 114 in the top right corner, so it is a page out of a larger file |
| 3 to 4 | University of Colorado, Department of Physics and Astrophysics, **27 January 1967**, Robert J. Low to Lt. Col. Robert Hippler, Headquarters USAF, The Pentagon. Signed "Bob", typed "Robert J. Low", "cc: E. U. Condon" |
| 5 | A clipping from the Elmira, N.Y. *Star-Gazette* of Thursday 26 January 1967 quoting Condon on the study. Not drafted below: see NOT REACHED |

**Why this matters.** Agent 1 recorded two witnesses describing this memo from opposite ends
and neither producing it: Michels on 19 October 2024 at 0:39:52, and Salas on 12 July 2025
at 0:37:53, whose caption renders the colonel as "Robert Hitler". The document exists. It is
signed by **Lt Col Robert R. Hippler**, which confirms the triage file's reconstruction of
that caption. The token "Hippler" appears nowhere in `research/*.json` and nowhere on the
board, so all three records below are additive.

**Where the record and the transcript differ, and it is the finding.** Michels said the memo
tells Condon "to show that UFO research was a waste of money". The letter does not put it
that way. It opens by calling itself "an informal letter" that "does not constitute the
formal letter requested by John Coleman", and says explicitly that it expresses Hippler's
"own opinion" and "is not binding on you". What it then does is name the answer wanted: the
cost to the taxpayer, and the Air Force getting out from under the programme. The disclaimer
and the instruction are on the same two pages, and a record that carries one without the
other misrepresents the document in either direction.

**Two things this is NOT.** It is not the 9 August 1966 Low trick memo, which the board
already carries. And it is not the handwritten Low note about a non-nuclear EMP explanation
that Salas says he holds and that agent 1 drafted at `robert-low`: that note is handwritten
and about Malmstrom, this one is typed and about the terms of the study. Do not let a later
pass merge them.
