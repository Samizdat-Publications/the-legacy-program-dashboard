## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

**S1 — Official classification following a joint investigation.** Per narration: after the
March 1971 incident and a subsequent mass sighting witnessed by ~150–200 base personnel, two
unnamed US servicemen and an English-speaking Spanish Air Force officer separately interviewed
and photographed each witness, instructed them to "forget" what they saw, and the case was
then sealed by the Spanish Ministry of Defense as a "reserved matter." (3 / 0:15:06–0:16:48)

**S2 — An unconnected second case, same pattern, no acknowledged link.** Per narration: a
12 Nov 1976 incident at Talavera la Real AB involved gunfire at a luminous humanoid figure and
a hospitalized soldier, officially confirmed only as far as "the gunfire occurred" — the
resemblance to the Roses case is asserted by the episode itself, not by any official record,
and "the official connection between Roses and Talavera was never made." (3 / 0:29:40–0:30:35)

**S3 — Distributed custody consolidated into one body.** Per John (file 4): prior to 1968–69,
NASA, the CIA and other agencies each held their own UFO-related files and staffed their own
personnel on the subject. Around 1968–69 this was reorganized: documentation and staff were
both moved to a centralized, informally-named body based at Area 51, with anyone in NASA, the
intelligence community or the military who had worked on the subject required to transfer
there too. (4 / 0:26:40–0:28:22)

**S4 — Centralization framed by the source himself as a disclosure-evasion mechanism.** John's
own stated belief: this is why no FOIA request has ever surfaced conclusive NASA-held UFO
material — the relevant records were removed from NASA's own vaults in the late 1960s and never
returned to individual agency custody. (4 / 0:28:22–0:29:14)

**S5 — Rogue retention and the missing title pages.** Per John: some individuals with access
did not comply with orders to surrender their files; some files went missing from vaults, some
were reported shredded but suspected preserved elsewhere, and some cleared personnel
"vanished" or were "found dead under questionable circumstances" — all asserted without names,
dates, or any sourcing beyond what John says he was told. Separately, every file he personally
handled had its original cover page torn off and replaced with a blank, pencil-titled page,
sourced (he was told) from the private collection of one individual — John's own guess is
**James Jesus Angleton**. (4 / 0:29:14–0:34:31)

**S6 — Physical compartmentalization by design.** Per John: transport into Area 51 involved
blacked-out aircraft and bus windows and goggles engineered to permit only a view of one's own
feet, specifically to prevent incidental viewing of anything outside one's clearance. (4 /
0:16:23–0:18:06)

**S7 — The source's own hedge.** John explicitly offers, unprompted, an alternative to his own
account: that the entire file collection he was shown may have been fabricated "test" material,
individually tailored so any leak could be traced back to him, given that his contract ran
exactly one year and he was never invited to extend it despite apparently satisfactory conduct.
This is recorded here as his own stated uncertainty, not as an separate claim of fact. (4 /
0:55:14–0:56:07)

  thread — the DNA testing of his hair sample — is a **private civilian** chain of custody

  to have seen or the DNA evidence chain.
