# Danny Jones corpus: the decision list (3 September 2026)

136 episodes of the Danny Jones Podcast, selected by title for UAP, intelligence, MK-ULTRA
and consciousness subjects, transcribed on Stewart's machine, mapped by 35 Sonnet agents
(`dannyjones-corpus-01..35.md`), cross-checked (981 confident names: 558 absent, 164 prose
only, 259 already nodes, on the tool as it stood that morning), triaged by six Opus agents
(`dannyjones-triage-1..6.md`), and 36 candidates opened by six Opus verification agents
(`verified-dannyjones-1..6.md`). This file is the orchestrating session's decision on each,
made under Stewart's standing approval ("I'm good with all decisions", 2 September; "go get
it", 3 September). Every row is reversible: the build lands in one research file and one
branch.

Verdict vocabulary as in the Michels and Area 52 lists: CONFIRMED (record opened and
supports the node), PARTLY (record opened, supports less than claimed), NOT OPENED (nothing
reached), CONTRADICTED (record says otherwise; kept as the finding).

About a third of this corpus was off-subject and the mappers said so; the structural yield
came from the MK-ULTRA episodes, the ex-officer interviews, the Jeremy Rys, Ryan Graves,
Annie Jacobsen, Danny Sheehan and David Morehouse episodes, and the James Fox episodes.
