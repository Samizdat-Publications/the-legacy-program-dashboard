## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the named speaker's own claims (or the book's, where
marked), several stated by Strieber himself as inference or speculation.

**S1 — Sarbacher/Battelle materials-analysis claim.** Strieber says Robert Sarbacher, identified
as a Battelle metallurgist, told him in the mid-1980s that Battelle had "made great strides"
understanding the Roswell metal (very thin, would not accept a bullet, self-restoring when
crumpled) and that keeping the work secret was, in Sarbacher's own words, holding humanity back.
Sarbacher urged Strieber to write down everything he knew before he forgot it. (01 / 1:44;14–1:46;54)

**S2 — A family custody-chain account.** Strieber's uncle, an Air Force intelligence officer,
says he was at Wright Field when the Roswell debris arrived under Gen. Twining; a friend,
"Erickson," later became Wright-Patterson's commanding officer and corroborated the account.
Both allegedly told Strieber, after *Communion* was published, that the debris contained "no
motor" — Strieber is not certain whether "balsa wood struts" was said verbatim or is his own
gloss. (01 / 0:51;01–0:52;45)

**S3 — An off-roster, still-funded military base.** Strieber says that during his own
questioning by the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence over his book *Majestic*, a senator
or staffer told him they had found a military base absent from the Pentagon's roster but still
being paid, whose personnel did not know they were not officially part of the US military.
Strieber offers no document, date or base name. (01 / 2:15;16)

**S4 — Missing Pentagon funds as the black budget.** Strieber's own inference, stated as such:
money the Pentagon "can't find" in its budget is the black-budget line funding a hidden
recovered-technology programme, distinct from black money from arms or drug trafficking. He
ties this to Richard Dolan's "breakaway civilization" concept. (01 / 2:16;16–2:17;15)

**S5 — Unofficial materials study at a named institute, no institutional record claimed.**
Strieber says he holds a fragment of layered bismuth-and-magnesium material and that Southwest
Research Institute in San Antonio studied it "unofficially, but with the permission of" its
director of materials science, whom he names only as "Bill Mallow." He offers no written report,
date or reference number. (01 / 2:02;39–2:04;45)

**S6 — Foreign/Axis-technology claim, third-hand.** Strieber relays Joseph Farrell's claim that
the Kecksburg, Pennsylvania object was "Die Glocke," German wartime technology, and separately
speculates (his own words, flagged as speculation) that a 1933 Italian crash of tall blond
humanoid bodies may explain Mussolini's contact with Germany. Neither claim carries a document
or archival citation. (01 / 2:13;52–2:14;59)

**S7 — The MacElroy book's custody-and-interrogation apparatus (low confidence, fictional
framing acknowledged by the host).** As the book tells it: a flight nurse with the 509th Bomb
Group, the sole person who can perceive the alien's telepathic communication, is made the
alien's designated interpreter/companion under a counterintelligence officer's (Cavitt's)
direction; a dedicated interview room is built with a stenographer, a foreign-language
interpreter, a code-breaking team, medical specialists and a psychologist, later fitted with a
one-way mirror for an observing "gallery." The book frames the alien's refusal to communicate
with anyone but the nurse as central to the whole narrative. This is recorded as a description
of the book's plot, not as an event. (02 / 0:32;02–0:33;42; 03 / 0:20;08)

**S8 — A separately-reported whistleblower document, named only in an aside.** Outside the
book's narrative, the host cites an interview of whistleblower Matt Brown by George Knapp and
Jeremy Corbell (on *Weaponized*), in which Brown allegedly references a document "now known,
presented to congress as" *Immaculate Constellation*, and is quoted (per the host, not verified
here) suggesting humanity may be treated as a resource, commodity, entertainment, or subject of
medical interest. (04 / 0:16;26–0:17;22)

## 4. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS SLICE

- **No AARO, Lockheed, DIA, CIA, NRO, Moon Dust, Blue Book, Blue Fly, Zodiac, AATIP or AAWSAP
  anywhere in files 01–04.** I checked the brief's keyword list against all four files. "SAP,"
  "FOIA," "hearing," "patent" and "NDAA" do not appear either.
- **"Program," "contract," "Pentagon," "Congress," "Senate" and "whistleblower" appear only in
  the handful of passages captured above** (S3, S4, S8) — the rest of files 01–04 is
  autobiographical contact narrative (file 01) or invented cosmology (files 02–04) excluded by
  the brief.
- **Files 03 and 04 in particular carry almost nothing structural.** The bulk of both is the
  book's own mythology — an "Isbe" spirit ontology, a "Domain" vs. "Old Empire" interstellar
  civilisation conflict, Earth as a prison planet, an alleged 1914 Archduke Franz Ferdinand
  possession-and-assassination story, and a reworking of Moses/Akhenaten/Egyptian religious
  history as alien manipulation. None of this is a claim about the US programme this board maps,
  and none of it is recorded above.
- **No Majestic-12 document content, no Wilson-Davis material, no Battelle-Wright-Patterson
  1949–55 metallurgy claim of the kind seen in the Gerb corpus** — Strieber's Sarbacher/Battelle
  claim (S1) is adjacent but distinct (a 1980s personal contact, not a documented programme
  history).
- **Both hosts of the Matilda MacElroy episodes are the same person as the Strieber episode
  (Chris Ramsay)**; there is no second voice in files 02–04 except the book being read aloud, so
  "speaker unclear" does not arise there — every claim in those three files is either the book's
  narrator/characters or Ramsay's own aside, and each is marked as such above.
