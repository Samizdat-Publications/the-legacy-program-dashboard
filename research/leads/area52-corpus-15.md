**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** Every line records *what a named
speaker says*, attributed, with a file + timestamp pointer. Nothing here is asserted as true

the pointer is given instead so the claim can be re-read at source.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper) with proper-noun corruption. Names below are marked:

  form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — I could not reconstruct it with confidence. Recorded raw.

Timestamps are the marker nearest the statement, format `H:MM:SS`, matching the transcripts'
own `**[H:MM:SS]**` markers.

## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

**S1 — A FOIA request stonewalled on a 1949 statute, offered by the filer as itself evidence
of something to hide.** James Fox describes filing (via associates and a retired USAF colonel)
a FOIA request for a roughly one-to-two-week window of USAF flight logs into Campinas around
January 1996; the CIA response neither confirmed nor denied per a cited 1949 national-security
provision. Fox and Chris Mellon (per Fox) both treat the stonewall itself as suspicious, given
the underlying claim was merely an unauthorized landing, not the alien case. (1 / 1:41:12–1:43:53)

**S2 — A named DIA source giving a partial, unconfirmed custody claim.** Fox states an
individual he identifies only as a DIA source told him the recovered remains "most likely
went ... on the east coast," declining to specify further. No document, name, or date is
attached to this claim. (1 / 1:44:42–1:45:42)

**S3 — A researcher's alleged compromise mid-investigation.** Ubijara Rodriguez, an early lead
UFO researcher and lawyer on the Varginha case, is said by the affected family to have later
appeared alongside American investigators (believed by the family to be from NASA) who told
them "we were never here, this never happened," after which Rodriguez stopped discussing the
case. Fox frames this as a case study in researcher compromise, not confirmed independently.
(1 / 1:31:58–1:33:36)

**S4 — An unverified leak naming real disclosure-community figures inside otherwise
unfalsifiable cosmology.** The anonymous 4chan/Reddit poster (file 4) claims, in a later
follow-up post, that **Luis Elizondo** "visits [Jeremy] Corbell's home to train him," that
Corbell is "one of the people within my reach for disclosure," and names an individual
"Stratton" [UNRESOLVED — possibly Jay Stratton, a real former AOIMSG/AARO-adjacent official,
reconstruction unconfirmed] as possibly responsible for a described crackdown. This is the
only place in file 4 where real, checkable names appear inside the leak text; the surrounding
material (quantum wave cosmology, "positive integers," an autonomous planetary "audit
facility") is explicitly self-flagged as unverifiable by both the poster and Ramsay.
(4 / 0:54:46–0:55:38)

## 6. WHAT IS *NOT* IN THIS SLICE

- File 4 is explicitly, repeatedly flagged as unverified by both its own anonymous author and
  by Ramsay ("larp or leak," "this isn't the gospel, this isn't the truth, this isn't fact").
  Almost all of its content is self-consistent invented cosmology (a "network," "quantum
