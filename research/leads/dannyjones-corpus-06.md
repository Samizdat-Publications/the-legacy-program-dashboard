## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the **named speaker's claims**, several explicitly
flagged by the speaker as their own inference.

**S1 — Correlated nuclear/UFO geography, asserted independently by two speakers.** Salas
(01) ties peak UFO activity to the start of US/Soviet nuclear testing in Nevada/New Mexico and
cites "that great map" showing detonations and sightings correlated over time (0:26:32). GSP
(02), independently, makes the same general claim and extends it to nuclear power plants —
Fukushima, Chernobyl, and a 1993 South African case near a nuclear site he could not fully name
(0:36:40–0:37:34).

**S2 — Simultaneous, precise missile disabling with no accepted mechanism.** Ten Minuteman
missiles at Oscar Flight went from green to red within seconds of each other during the 24
March 1967 sighting; Boeing's own investigation could reproduce a *disabling* signal in bench
tests but stated no known way to *deliver* it to 10 separately-shielded missiles at once. Salas
treats this gap as the core of his case. (01 / 0:56:34–1:00:11)

**S3 — Gag order under threat of imprisonment.** Salas and his commander were made to sign
NDAs on the spot, administered by an OSI officer, "on penalty of spending a lot of time" at
Fort Leavenworth; guards were separately instructed (by unknown means) not to speak to Salas
afterward. (01 / 0:41:14–0:42:50)

**S4 — Official study informed and silent.** Sylvania's Ray Fowler gave Condon's own
investigator names, phone numbers and details of the shutdowns; the 1968 Condon report does not
mention them. Salas frames this as evidence the study's outcome was predetermined. (01 /
1:00:11–1:05:59)

**S5 — A counter-narrative surfaced decades later, with a possible institutional source.**
The 2025 WSJ piece (D8) offers a mundane explanation Salas rejects on procedural grounds (an
EMP rig would have needed days of visible on-site construction the guards would have reported)
and technical grounds (per Salas's own ChatGPT/Google query, no mobile non-nuclear EMP weapon
existed in 1967). Danny separately claims one of the article's sources has a psyops background
at a major defense contractor (Northrop Grumman), per LinkedIn. (01 / 1:12:45–1:17:46)

**S6 — "The legacy program": private contractor custody of recovered material, deliberately
outside any single administration's full knowledge.** Salas's own claim, stated as his belief
rather than as sourced fact: major aerospace companies hold access to downed craft and parts;
even the sitting president "may not even" be told everything, because doing so could expose
crimes. (01 / 1:26:41–1:27:29)

**S7 — Private-sector custody as a deliberate FOIA evasion.** Salas, in response to a
question about how private companies would be "shielded from the law," agrees this is roughly
how it works: pushing custody of the material into private hands removes it from Freedom of
Information Act reach. Presented as his own inference. (01 / 1:37:06–1:37:59)

**S8 — A coordinating body above individual national governments.** Salas states he is
"convinced" there is an "international UFO cabal" — powerful people who control use of the
secret and agree collectively on what their national governments disclose. His own framing,
not sourced. (01 / 1:38:48–1:39:49)

**S9 — Institutional precedent for large-scale secret harm to US citizens, offered by Zamilov
as the more parsimonious alternative to a suppressed-technology narrative.** LSD testing,
forced sterilization, and — specifically — irradiation experiments on US servicemen ("nuclear
soldiers," made to stare at test detonations) and on disabled children, the latter allegedly
publicly acknowledged in some form by Bill Clinton. Zamilov's point is explicitly that this
kind of secrecy is more plausible than either alien visitation or a suppressed physics
breakthrough. (04 / 2:41:25–2:43:12)

## 4. THE MISSING/DEAD DEFENSE-LINKED SCIENTISTS SEGMENT (File 04)

This is the one passage in files 02–04 that squarely intersects both this board's subject and
the wider project's brief (missing individuals connected to defense contractors). Recorded
here in full detail because of that overlap; nothing below is independently corroborated —
it is Danny reading an on-screen Daily Mail article (D10) and Zamilov reacting to it live.

- The article, dated 11 April 2026, is described on screen as documenting a pattern of "deaths
  and disappearances in recent years" among people "linked to America's nuclear secrets."
- **Stephen Garcia, 48**, is named as the most recent: vanished without trace 28 August 2025,
  last seen in Albuquerque, New Mexico, "carrying only a handgun."
- The host paraphrases further entries without reading full names for most: an unnamed "top
  scientist detecting signs of alien life at NASA JPL"; **Frank Mywald** [UNRESOLVED]; "two
  people from Los Alamos Labs"; "two people linked to" Wright-Patterson AFB.
- Both host and guest editorialise that "they're all dead or missing," but the transcript gives
  no consolidated count, no cause-of-death information, and no citation trail from the article
  itself beyond the headline and byline.
- Separately, in the same conversational stretch, Danny and Zamilov discuss an unnamed scientist
  believed killed by Mossad, described as having built a "super gun" for Saddam Hussein and as
  having been killed in or near Canada, in front of his apartment. **No name is given for this
  person in the transcript** — do not assume an identity for them from outside knowledge.
- Zamilov's own conclusion, stated plainly, is that he does *not* believe scientists are being
  killed to suppress a genuine technological discovery; he offers no alternative explanation for
  the disappearance pattern itself, only for why he doubts the "suppressed breakthrough" reading
  of it.
