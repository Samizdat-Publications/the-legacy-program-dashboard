# Danny Jones corpus, triage of group 6 (lead maps 31 to 35)

**Scope.** Lead maps `dannyjones-corpus-31.md` through `-35.md` (batch 35 holds one
episode), and the 61 ABSENT names in `dannyjones-absent/group-6.tsv`. Nothing below is
asserted as true and nothing is proposed for the board. Section A rows name a record to
be opened; the record, not the speaker, is the thing that would justify a node.

Citation form in every row: `map / episode / timestamp`, taken from the map's own tables.

Counts: A 18 names in 15 rows, B 8 names plus 7 carried resolutions, C 3 false ABSENTs
plus 6 node notes, D 32 names in 6 groups. Batch 35 (ep 232) produced no ABSENT name at
all: everything structural in it was already a node or already in board prose.
