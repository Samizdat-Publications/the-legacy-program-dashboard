**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** This is not board content and
nothing here is asserted as true. Every row records *what a named speaker claims*,

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper), uncorrected, and corrupt names silently and plausibly. Names below are

  caption form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** -- I could not reconstruct it with confidence. Recorded raw either way.

Timestamps are the `**[H:MM:SS]**` marker nearest the statement, matching the source files.

## 5. THE AL O'DONNELL / ROSWELL DISPUTE -- two accounts of the same claim

This is the one place in the batch where two guests, in two different episodes, discuss the
same named source and directly disagree. Recorded side by side rather than merged.

**TD Barnes' position (415, 0:58:03--1:08:44 and 1:41:14--1:50:08):** Barnes says he knows
"Al" (not otherwise named by Barnes in this episode) personally and disputes Annie
Jacobsen's *Area 51* broadly: he says her book conflates the CIA/DoD-run Area 51 with the
separate Atomic Energy Commission/Department of Energy-run nuclear-test-site programme next
door ("apples and oranges... those guys were never at Area 51"); that DOE personnel were
"never allowed on the place"; and specifically that her EG&G-engineer source (whom Barnes
calls "the M-Dong Bell" [UNRESOLVED phrase, unclear if nickname or transcription error) told
her a "wild story" as a joke after she repeatedly pressed him for something "juicy," and that
the source later told Barnes so directly. Barnes separately recounts a claim that an ABC
Nightline producer asked Jacobsen on-air why she published an unverifiable claim and that she
answered "because he said it, I'm allowed to use it."

**Annie Jacobsen's position (417, 1:44:09--2:06:15):** Jacobsen names her source in full as
**Alfred "Al" O'Donnell**, describes him as a genuinely credentialed nuclear "trigger man"
(armed/wired/fired 186 nuclear tests, profiled by name in the Las Vegas Review-Journal,
listed among "100 most important people in Las Vegas"), says she spent "100 days" with him
over years, that he had an FBI minder, and that his story to her -- that in the 1950s he was
one of five EG&G engineers tasked with reverse-engineering a Soviet "War of the Worlds"-style
hoax craft with disfigured/surgically altered small human beings inside, intended to
overwhelm US early-warning radar and provoke panic -- was given to her fully on the record,
with his family present at points, and that he asked her to withhold the story's most painful
detail (a personal confession tied to his grandchild's death) until he judged the time right.
She says he told George Knapp a reversed version of the same story (claiming the beings
really were extraterrestrial) specifically to get Knapp "off his back," and that after the
book published, an FBI-identified "minder" ordered her to cease all contact with O'Donnell;
she was later notified of his death by the same minder and was refused permission to attend
the funeral.

**Neither speaker's account is preferred here.** Both are recorded as claims about the same
named individual and should be checked against each other and against any independent
record of Alfred O'Donnell.
