`[2:03:47]` of the 31 May 2025 episode. `research/leads/michels-corpus-10.md` section 4 records

a different name from `2:41:23` onward, and the map deliberately leaves the identity unresolved.

reliably named, so the claim cannot be written in the required form. Left out until the
published episode credits settle who was in the chair.

2025 episode the same unresolved speaker names "Arnie Arleson" as "the actual commander of the

instruction is the right one and it should stay in the lead map rather than becoming a board
