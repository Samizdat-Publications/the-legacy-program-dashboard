   OMB Form 171 he handed a researcher in 2015, and that researcher's first pass through the
   public record found him as "Quality Control Branch Manager", "Quality Assurance Staff", under

   career he described.** Philip J. Klass obtained the record from the National Personnel
   Records Center in St. Louis and printed the assignments in *SUN* #68, Summer 2001: clerk/typist

   detail that does the real damage, because it is Stone's own act and not an absence: on **23

   Bentwaters incident, **from his home address and without identifying himself as a serving
   Army sergeant**, three years after the date on which he says his Army team was sent there to
   investigate it.

   their own names, and the reader can read both.** Jacobs in the *MUFON UFO Journal* No. 249,

   confirms Jacobs's unit, his role and his presence, and denies his interpretation.** That is

5. **The trap the ARV build flagged is real and it generalises.** Appearing in the April 2001

   session.** Every proposed node below therefore separates "gave testimony, dated D" from

## 1. The anchor and the event

### 1.1 The Briefing Document

The document's own title page reads: "DISCLOSURE PROJECT BRIEFING DOCUMENT / Prepared for:
Members of the Press, Members of United States Government, Members of the US Scientific
Community / Written and Compiled by: Steven M. Greer, M.D., Director and Theodore C. Loder III,
Ph.D. / April, 2001". Greer's address is printed as "The Disclosure Project, PO Box 265, Crozet,
VA 22932".

Section 3.8, "Summary of Video Taped Witness Testimony and Government Documents", runs from the
document's printed page 48 to page 349 and contains **68 dated testimony headings**, each of the
form "Testimony of [name, rank, service]" followed on the next line by a month and year between
**July 2000 and March 2001**, with three exceptions carrying earlier dates (Mitchell May 1998,
Alexeyev March 1997) or no date at all (Cooper, McKenzie, Corso).

**This is a Tier 1 record of an act of testimony and nothing else.** It is the interested party's
own compilation, the interviews are conducted by Greer himself with his initials printed in the
transcripts ("SG: Dr. Steven Greer"), and Greer's editorial interjections are printed inside the
testimony marked "SG]". A claim that cites it for anything other than "X said this, on this date,
under this heading" is miscited.

### 1.2 The 9 May 2001 National Press Club press conference

**Date and venue: Wednesday 9 May 2001, the National Press Club, Washington DC.** Confirmed by
two independent contemporaneous sources with opposite positions:

## 9. The rest of the roster, and why a set node

The canon audit's own proposal was one set node carrying the remaining witnesses. **This pass
endorses that and widens it**, because after section 7 and section 8.2 the evidence pattern is
clear: for a large majority of these sixty-eight names, the Briefing Document is the *only*
record, and a person node built on it would carry exactly one claim, of one tier, pointing at one
URL. That is a node that asserts "somebody said something" and nothing else, and the board already
knows from section 6 of `CLAUDE.md` what happens when `witness-chain` edges become the dominant
edge type of a cluster.

**The set node is the honest shape.** It records the fact that sixty-eight people gave dated
testimony to one editor in one nine-month window, it carries the roster, and it lets individual
nodes be promoted out of it **only when an independent record is found**, which is exactly what
happened here for Jacobs and Callahan.
