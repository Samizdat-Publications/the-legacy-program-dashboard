## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are **the named speaker's claims**, several explicitly
flagged by the speaker themselves as inference or "smoke, no flame."

**S1 — DARPA as Corso's alleged distribution conduit.** Blitch, to fellow DARPA scientists at a

distribute them throughout the... free market economy." Blitch calls this "some smoke," not
flame. (04 / 2:42:11–2:43:51)

managed at what would be Brigadier-General/PEO level elsewhere. (04 / 2:45:31–2:47:09)

capability already existed elsewhere in the programme structure. (04 / 2:27:48–2:29:33,
2:47:09–2:50:29)
