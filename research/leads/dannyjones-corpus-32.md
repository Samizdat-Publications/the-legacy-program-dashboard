**Source:** `dist/transcripts/md/`, the four files named for batch 32 in
`research/leads/dannyjones-batches.txt`.
**Extracted:** 3 September 2026.
**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** Every line records what a named
SPEAKER says, attributed, with a file + timestamp pointer. Nothing here is asserted as
true and nothing here is graded. No quotation exceeds a short phrase; where the wording
matters the pointer is given instead so the claim can be re-read at source.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper), uncorrected, and corrupt proper nouns silently and plausibly. Names
below are marked:

- **[CONF]** -- spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure,
  organisation, document or programme I already know exists.
- **[PHON?]** -- phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw
  transcript form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** -- I could not reconstruct it with confidence, or could not verify it
  independently even though the transcript spells it consistently. Recorded raw.

Timestamps are the `**[H:MM:SS]**` marker nearest the statement.

**Attribution.** 212, 214 and 215 are host-plus-guest(s) conversations (Danny Jones is the
host throughout; an off-mic producer "Steve" pulls up images/text in 212 and 215 and is
not a speaker of substance). 213 is host-plus-guest. A claim is attributed to the guest
only when the passage makes clear the guest is speaking in their own voice; a story a
guest tells about a third party (e.g. Ryan Bledsoe reporting what "Jim Simivan" or "Tim"
told him) is recorded as the guest's own report of that conversation, not as the third
party's own testimony.
