**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper), uncorrected, and corrupt names silently and plausibly. Names below are
marked:

- **[CONF]** — spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure, organisation,
  document or programme I already know exists.
- **[PHON?]** — phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw caption
  form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — I could not reconstruct it with confidence, or the name reads clean but
  is not a figure I can independently confirm. Recorded raw either way.

Timestamps are the caption marker nearest the statement, format `H:MM:SS`, matching the

## 4. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. Attributed to the speaker named, not asserted as true.

**S1 — The Roswell/Wright-Patterson/EBE narrative, recited by John Lear on camera, is
near-identical to the 1987 Paranet document already logged from this corpus's batch 01.** Same
custody chain (crash, recovery, Wright-Patterson, weather-balloon cover story), same named
surgeon (Bronk), same EBE numbering convention. Treat any future sourcing of this material as
one claim repeated by one speaker across two recorded appearances, not two corroborating
sources. (07 / 1:27:42–1:29:19; cf. `area52-corpus-01.md` §2 D1, §3, §4 S1–S11)

**S2 — Presidential briefing as need-to-know, for plausible deniability.** Spidey's own stated
inference, not sourced to any document: presidents are "temporary employees" briefed only
partially by career personnel "at NSA, CIA, NASA ... NRO," specifically so that if pressed later
("did you know about this?") a president can truthfully say no. Offered as a general theory to
explain why Trump, Obama and Bush all describe hearing about UAP only secondhand, from pilots or
staff, rather than from primary access. (07 / 0:29:53)

**S3 — Canadian custody and disclosure pattern, Feb 2023.** An object downed over Yukon
airspace; image prepared for near-immediate public release, then withheld on the Department of
National Defense's own stated national-security/confusion grounds; later released under an FOI
request; the on-the-ground recovery attempt was called off, reason given as terrain too rugged
for physical recovery. Ramsay notes this leaves the object's actual disposition (recovered vs.
still in place) unconfirmed either way. (08 / 0:05:36–0:08:09)

**S4 — A named case designation exists for at least one of the 2023 North American
takedowns.** "UAP 23," per Ramsay, is the official number given to the Yukon object as the 23rd
sighting logged during that period's spate of intercepts. No issuing office is named for the
designation itself. (08 / 0:09:50)
