**Source:** `dist/transcripts/md/`, four files assigned to batch 30.

**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** Every line records *what a named
speaker claims* (Jesse Michels, his guest, or a person he quotes/summarises), attributed,
with a file + timestamp pointer. Nothing here is asserted as true and nothing here is
graded. No quotation exceeds a short phrase; where the wording matters the pointer is given
instead so the claim can be re-read at source.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper, uncorrected). Names below are marked:

- **[CONF]** — spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure/organisation/
  document/programme.
- **[PHON?]** — phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw
  caption form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — I could not reconstruct it with confidence. Recorded raw.

Timestamps are the `[H:MM:SS]` marker nearest the statement, matching this corpus's own
paragraph markers.

## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — custody, secrecy, compartmentalisation

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the speaker's claims, several explicitly their
own inference.

**S1 — Custody/clearance lineage, Manhattan Project to DOE.** Vallee's claim, restated in
file 02 quoting Vallee directly: recovered material would pass from Manhattan Project custody
into the AEC, then into the DOE, each with its own separate clearance line that does not roll
up to the executive branch — which is why people he knows holding clearances above top secret
have never heard of Trinity. (01 / 0:08:29; 02 / 0:04:09–0:05:52)

**S2 — The Wilson memo's compartmentalisation claim.** A black-budget private aerospace
corporation reverse-engineering material its own gatekeepers believe is not of this earth,
with little success since Roswell, structured so no one below the top holds the whole
picture. (01 / 0:09:32–0:10:37)

**S3 — Chris Mellon's near-miss.** A scheduled meeting with an aerospace-company contact
allegedly directly involved in the programme was pre-empted by the contact's reported death
roughly two weeks out. (01 / 0:08:29–0:09:32)

**S4 — The 1954 Atomic Energy Act as a UFO-secrecy vehicle.** Grusch's claim (via Michels)
that the Act's material-secrecy definition was written broadly enough to cover any
radiological emission, not only fissile material, extending nuclear-grade secrecy to UFO
material by the same mechanism. Cross-reference `gerb-corpus-01-12.md` S9 — the same
underlying claim, independently present in a different Michels episode. (02 / 0:04:09–0:05:52)

**S5 — Manhattan Project as proto-programme.** Grusch's claim that the Manhattan Project
served as "the first blue book," collecting UFO reports, with unnamed colleagues' grandparents
allegedly among the report-takers. (02 / 0:05:52–0:06:46)

**S6 — The Condon blackmail inference.** Michels' own inference, explicitly flagged as such:
Condon's 1966 UFO-debunking role may connect to an FBI dossier Guy Hottel built on him during
a 1950s loyalty investigation, given his clearance was restored the same year he was placed on
the panel that ended federal UFO research funding. Not sourced to a document. (02 /
0:06:46–0:09:16)

**S7 — Townsend Brown's technology into deep black aerospace.** Unsourced inference chain:
Brown fired by the Navy in 1942, appears at "Martin Vega Corporation" [PHON?] roughly a year
before Skunk Works forms, and the B-2 bomber allegedly uses his propulsion principle;
Lockheed Martin's own statement in response to Grusch's testimony is read as a non-denial.
(02 / 0:09:16–0:11:01)

**S8 — Wright-Patterson access denial.** Goldwater sought access to a room at Wright-Patterson
and was refused by LeMay. Cross-reference `gerb-corpus-01-12.md` (file 03, 06;34) — same claim,
consistent presentation across two independent Michels episodes. (02 / 0:11:01–0:11:55)

**S9 — Corso's covert recovery group.** A group under Hillenkoetter [PHON?] tasked with
collecting off-planet-technology intelligence and retrieving Roswell debris; Corso claims
personal participation in the cleanup and downstream technology transfer (fiber optics,
lasers, integrated circuits, Kevlar). (01 / 0:16:22–0:17:28)

**S10 — Nuclear-site correlation as evidentiary base.** Hastings' Malmstrom-rooted research,
167 military sources, offered as the reason to take a UFO/nuclear-site correlation seriously
rather than dismiss it as a psy-op. Cross-reference `gerb-corpus-01-12.md` D13/D14 (Malmstrom
affidavits, the 1975 NORAD log) for the same site and adjacent claims. (02 / 0:03:15–0:04:09)

**S11 — The JFK letter, self-flagged as unverified.** Michels presents D10 and the E. Howard
Hunt claim as a possible link between JFK's assassination and UFO inquiries, but explicitly
asks his audience to verify or falsify the letter rather than asserting it. (02 /
0:11:55–0:12:45)

**S12 — "Exploit, don't fabricate" argument.** Michels uses the Walter Bedell Smith (1953) and
Allen Dulles letters as his best evidence against a total-fabrication theory: both treat the
phenomenon as real and militarily/psychologically useful, not invented. (02 / 0:12:45–0:13:42)
