## 2. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered for citation. All are the **named speaker's claims**, several explicitly flagged
by the speaker himself as inference or as sourced only to an anonymous contact.

**S1 — A WWII-origin reverse-engineering lineage, stated as inference.** Jorjani's own
chain, given explicitly as his reasoning rather than as sourced fact: Roswell (1947) plus
the Magenta, Italy crash Grusch describes (1933) gave the US "a lot of time" by 1953 to
analyse wreckage and reverse-engineer "basic propulsion principles" — which he ties to the
1953 aerospace-company electro-gravitics claims (S3) and to Louis/Edward Witten. (248 /
2:36:14)

**S2 — An unsourced but twice-corroborated claim of an active Lockheed reverse-engineering
and pilot-training facility.** Jorjani states this rests on two separate unnamed contacts,
one of whom showed him a physical training artefact (a 1960s-era gyroscopic time-dilation
demonstrator) he was not permitted to photograph. He is explicit that he will not name the
source. (248 / 1:36:42–1:41:37)

**S3 — A public, on-the-record 1953 claim of electro-gravitic propulsion in development,**
independent of any UAP claim: named aerospace-company executives (Martin, Bell, Convair)
told mainstream press they could deliver such propulsion at Manhattan-Project-scale
funding. Jorjani treats the subsequent public silence on this line of research as itself
evidence of a black-program capture, not merely commercial failure. (248 / 2:34:20–2:35:18)

**S4 — A named faction shaping USG interpretation and disclosure framing on religious
lines.** The Collins Elite, per Jorjani, has penetrated "all of the military intelligence
services" and civilian CIA, not from a single directive but from a shared 1950s-origin
Christian-evangelical conviction that the phenomenon is angelic/demonic; he treats this as
the reason the "non-human intelligence" framing preferred by current disclosure advocates
gets contested. (248 / 2:41:48–2:43:38)

**S5 — Disinformation used against a UAP researcher as a documented tactic, with a stated
Soviet-penetration rationale.** The Bennewitz/Doty case, as Jorjani frames it: an Air Force
investigator confirmed a witness's claims in order to discredit him and the broader
community by association, motivated (per Jorjani) by USG concern that the UAP research
community itself was penetrated by Soviet intelligence. (248 / 3:01:40–3:02:33)

**S6 — European contactee cults as a stated disclosure-softening contingency.** Citing
Vallée's *Messengers of Deception*, Jorjani says high-level officials in Spain and Italy
were involved with UFO contact cults specifically to soften the impact of eventual
disclosure on those countries' Catholic populations. No document or named official is
given. (248 / 3:00:49)
