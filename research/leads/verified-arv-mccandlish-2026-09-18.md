**The seven findings that matter, in order:**

1. **The "SORENSEN 1990 INTERVIEW" on arvp.co.uk is a real document and it is better than its
   billing.** It is 31 scanned pages: handwritten notes on *Aviation Week & Space Technology*
   letterhead over the name "William B. Scott, Senior Engineering Editor" with a McGraw-Hill
   logo, followed by a 16 page typed transcript headed "NOTES FROM INTERVIEW ON 12-3-90 WITH
   'BRAD'" and "COMPILED 12-11-90; SCOTT/AVWK LANCASTER". One handwritten sheet is headed
   "Mark - 8/13/90". A handwritten addendum on the last page reads "As of 12/10/90 - additional
   info from Brad". This is the first-person source the Danny Jones corpus row D4 says Gerb
   located, and it is now open and read.
2. **The document contradicts the Frank Carlucci identification, and relocates its origin.**
   Scott's notes have Brad describing his host as a man who "was in the Carter administration,
   and they wanted him on the Bush team", "he's a Democrat, which is not in keeping with the
   Bush admin. at all", who "Runs a business of injection molding and special materials...has
   about 6 businesses". Frank Carlucci was the sitting Secretary of Defense on 12 November 1988.
   Separately, the board's triage calls Carlucci "a researcher's guess at two removes"; the
   opened 2001 Briefing Document shows it is McCandlish's own published speculation, in his own
   voice: "this fellow whom I speculate was probably Frank Carlucci."
3. **The congressmen are not in the primary document.** The Gerb corpus has Sorensen naming
   Sen. Alan Cranston and Rep. George E. Brown Jr. as present. In Scott's notes Brad says the
   opposite about his own knowledge of who was there: "we didn't get in on the introductions of
   people", "I did NOT see any stars in there--like generals of any sort", and, asked who put
   the event on, "Me too; look, we got there a little late". No opened record names Cranston or
   Brown at the exhibit.
4. **The coroner's file is two documents, not one, and one of them limits the other.** The
   Verdict of Death (without jury), signed 21 June 2021 by Chief Deputy Coroner Logan
   Stonehouse, gives manner of death as Suicide. The Report of External Examination by Dan
   Purtzer MD, dated 19 April 2021 at 0713, carries on its second page: "Due to the obvious
   cause of death, internal examination is not performed." Anyone arguing either way about that
   death should be arguing over an external examination.
5. **The age in the coroner's file is 68, which rules out two of the birth years in
   circulation.** The Verdict reads "a 68 year old male" and the external examination "appears
   his stated age of 68-years". The arvp.co.uk research page gives "(1957 - 2021)". Both cannot
   be right.
6. **The profession question is settled against the owner's belief.** "I work principally as a
   conceptual artist. Most of my clients are in the defense industry. I occasionally work
   directly for the military, but most the time I work for civilian corporations that are
   defense contractors". He is an illustrator who drew what somebody else described. That is
   what makes him notable and it is not a claim to technical authority.
7. **The arvp.co.uk figures do not match either primary source.** The site says "one with a
   130-foot diameter, another with a 60-foot diameter, and a smaller one measuring 24 feet".
   Scott's 1990 notes: "The small one was like 20 ft, the next 60 ft. diameter and the next
   one,...maybe 120 ft. diam. Just a guess; don't hold me to that." The 2001 testimony: 24 ft,
   60 ft, and "about 120 to 130 feet". The site has hardened a witness's own disclaimed guess
   into a figure.

## 7. Contradictions and the case against

**7.1 The claimed VIP is not Frank Carlucci, on the primary document's own description.** The
1990 notes give the host as a man who "was in the Carter administration, and they wanted him
on the Bush team", who "is a very organized, straightforward, honest, etc. guy. In fact, he's
a Democrat, which is not in keeping with the Bush admin. at all", and who "Runs a business of
injection molding and special materials...has about 6 businesses." Sorensen describes him as a
friend and fellow pilot: "I'm just a good friend, and we both love airplanes. I'm a pilot,
he's a pilot...buddies in that sense." McCandlish's 2001 description is different again: "a
tall, thin, white-haired man with glasses [and] an Italian-sounding last name", "already a
millionaire in his own right and was in civilian life again after having been either a
Secretary of Defense or an Under-Secretary of Defense", followed by "this fellow whom I
speculate was probably Frank Carlucci". Carlucci was the sitting Secretary of Defense on 12
November 1988, so "in civilian life again" does not fit him on that date. **The board's
existing triage calls the Carlucci identification "a researcher's guess at two removes". The
opened record corrects that in one direction and confirms it in another: the guess is the
relayer's own, published in 2001 in his own voice and labelled as speculation, and it is then
re-attributed to researchers by later carriers.** On the Jesse Michels episode recorded in
`michels-corpus-02.md` file 03, Matthew Pines attributes the identification to "a couple of
really good researchers". Nothing opened here supports the identification and the earliest
document cuts against it.

**7.2 The congressmen are not in the primary document.** `gerb-corpus-25-35.md` records Gerb
saying Sorensen named Sen. Alan Cranston and Rep. George E. Brown Jr. as present, and that a
male staffer in Brown's office allegedly confirmed the exhibit (file 33 at 00;00;28 / 00;07;35
/ 00;25;14). In the 1990 notes Sorensen says he missed the introductions, saw no general
officers, and did not know who was running the event. No opened record names either
legislator at Norton on 12 November 1988.

**7.3 The sizes have drifted, and the drift runs one way.** 1990: "20 ft, the next 60 ft.
diameter and the next one,...maybe 120 ft. diam. Just a guess; don't hold me to that." 2001:
24 ft, 60 ft, "about 120 to 130 feet". 2024, on arvp.co.uk: "one with a 130-foot diameter,
another with a 60-foot diameter, and a smaller one measuring 24 feet in diameter", with no
disclaimer. Each retelling is more precise than the last about a figure the witness explicitly
refused to stand behind.

**7.4 The witness disowns part of the drawing.** Shown McCandlish's sketches in 1990, Sorensen
says of the manned vehicle "this is probably the farthest from what I saw", and "mark
extrapolates a bit too much". The board should not carry the drawing as a rendering of
Sorensen's account without carrying this line beside it.

**7.5 The nursery-rhyme names are misreported in the corpus.** `dannyjones-corpus-20.md` row
D4 gives the three craft as "Little Bear/Mama Bear/Papa Bear". The document says "daddy bear,
mommy bear, baby bear".

**7.6 Aviation Week did not publish the account.** The interview was never written up; the
document is a working file, and the witness had refused to talk until McCandlish intervened.
`gerb-corpus-25-35.md` records Gerb reading Scott's own stated reason as disbelief (file 33 at
00;22;44 / 00;30;16). **That reading of Scott's motive was NOT verified here**: Scott's stated
reservations inside the document are about the witness's physics and about his fee, not a
stated refusal to publish.

**7.7 Eric Davis, from the other side of the classification line, says it is false.**
`michels-corpus-03.md` records, from the American Alchemy episode "CIA Scientist: 'George Bush
Was Briefed on UFOs' (ft. Eric Davis)" at 0:36:24 to 0:38:00, that Davis says no ARV exists
and none has ever been built, that Northrop Grumman and Area 51 ARV rumours are "all myth",
that they originate with Bob Lazar-type storytellers who "believe their own bullshit" rather
than with deliberate disinformation, and that the CIA "shut it all down" in 1989. **The
episode's publication date is not recorded in the corpus file and was not established here**,
so a build session must supply it before writing the Tier 2 claim in the required
"On <show>, <date>, <speaker> said" form.

**7.8 Sorensen is hostile to the researchers who carry his account.**
`gerb-corpus-25-35.md` records Gerb saying Sorensen told him McCandlish "died because he could
not keep his mouth shut", that he "protected him until I could not", and warned Gerb off (file
33 at 00;21;41 / 01;05;39). **Not verified here**: no record of that exchange was opened.

**7.9 The coroner's file is not the strong document either side wants.** It rules suicide, and
it rests on an external examination that expressly declined an internal one. It is the best
record available and it is thinner than the argument built on it in both directions.
