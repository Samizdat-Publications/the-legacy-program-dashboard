## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, custody, classification, retaliation

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are **the speaker's claims**, several explicitly
their own inference.

**S1 — "Documents first" as an investigative doctrine.** Sheehan: any real investigation of
UAP secrecy should subpoena the black-budget documents (current and historical, covering
unacknowledged SAPs) before subpoenaing people — names follow "organically" once the
documents are in hand, because the documents lead to the contractors and the contractors'
liaisons inside government. (02 / 2:18:37–2:21:12)

**S2 — Records denial escalates to physical retaliation.** Sheehan, discussing the FBI raid
on the Church of Scientology: the search warrant was, in his own legal assessment, "an
absolute general warrant," struck down by a federal judge after Sheehan obtained a
restraining order and had all seized materials, fingerprints and photographs returned.
(02 / 1:11:13–1:14:01)

**S3 — A named custody/oversight chain for disclosure, deliberately spanning all three
federal branches.** Sheehan: the disclosure bill's nine-person review panel is appointed by
the president (executive) but its subpoena power runs through the Attorney General to a
federal judge (judicial), while Congress (legislative) holds its own separate task-force
subpoena power via Comer's committee — a design Sheehan calls deliberate, so no single branch
can unilaterally block it. (02 / 2:22:00–2:22:49)

**S4 — A programme causing documented harm to its own personnel, that continues regardless.**
Sheehan: "psionic assist" technology has killed American test pilots; the operators'
position is that military personnel accept the risk, and the programme continues despite the
deaths. He says he is now coordinating with DOJ Criminal Division staff who refused
administration-mandated resignation letters to investigate. (02 / 3:11:42–3:14:24)

**S5 — Aerospace-contractor patent incentive as a disclosure obstacle.** Sheehan's own
inference: aerospace contractors are fighting the disclosure bill because they want to
secure patents on reverse-engineered technology and be paid for access to it, technology
Sheehan argues is not rightfully theirs to patent because "it's been recovered from the
crafts." (02 / 2:08:52–2:09:50)

**S6 — Congressional-district geography as political obstruction, echoed from the prior
corpus.** Sheehan names Rep. Mike Turner's district (Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio's 10th) as
the reason he blocked the House floor vote — the same custody-hub/political-geography
pattern recorded from the Gerb corpus pass (there as "S19"). (02 / 2:07:01–2:08:52)

**S7 — Career retaliation against psychic-programme veterans described as structurally
identical to nuclear-site witness retaliation elsewhere in this corpus.** Not directly
stated by Sheehan as a parallel, but the pattern he describes — pilots harmed, deaths
downplayed, the programme continuing regardless — matches the retaliation pattern already
on the board from the Michels corpus (batch 04, S6). Recorded as an editorial cross-link,
not Sheehan's own claim. (02 / 3:11:42–3:14:24)

**S8 — Alleged corporate/church intelligence-adjacent activity outside government
structures.** Sheehan's account of Scientology's Guardian's Office running its own
remote-viewing intelligence programme (separate from, but overlapping with, Puthoff/Targ's
government-linked SRI programme) is the most explicit non-state actor described anywhere in
this batch as running something resembling an intelligence collection capability.
(02 / 1:14:55–1:24:04)

**S9 — Mass telepathic influence, tested and functional at a claimed 80% compliance rate.**
Sheehan, from the same file set as D5/D6: presents this not as a hypothetical but as a
demonstrated capability from a specific dated test. If true, this is the single most
consequential capability claim anywhere in this batch, and Sheehan offers no document number
for it. (02 / 1:22:24–1:24:04)
