## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, funding, custody, oversight

All are the **named speaker's claims**, several explicitly flagged by the speaker as their own inference.

**S1 — Skywatcher's founding event.** 2021, a government-contracted war-game (blue team/red team counter-drone exercise); an intern's camera operator spotted a seven-craft "Class 1" formation maneuvering above the site, invisible to the naked eye and unheard by nearby personnel; initially treated as classified US technology and protected under informal OPSEC/NDA discipline before the team understood otherwise. (3 / 0:08:35–0:12:05)

**S2 — Contracting mismatch.** Guest states Skywatcher has been contracted by the government for events "unrelated to UAPs" where "all they really wanted was UAP data," and that no direct UAP-collection contract has ever been offered to his knowledge, to him or to anyone else. (3 / 0:19:08–0:20:03)

**S3 — A claimed UAP energy-weapon response, c. 2003.** During a government-contracted event, a team member aimed a directed-energy device at a "Class 6" UAP; before he completed the turn, the object allegedly emitted an energy pulse that shut down three separate, non-networked electronic systems simultaneously. Guest treats this as the clearest example of the phenomenon reacting to team intent rather than to sensor exposure. Not repeated since; guest says repeating it now would be a federal offence. (3 / 1:23:26–1:26:44)

**S4 — Government SME "off the record" admission.** In a classified briefing, an unnamed subject-matter expert reportedly told the guest, smirking, that a body of UAP data matched nothing in any known US inventory and might be "off-planet" — offered as evidence that some government scientists privately entertain non-human origin even while publicly guarded. (3 / 0:42:18–0:43:06)

**S5 — Suspected information-operations campaign against Skywatcher.** Guest attributes a wave of coordinated negative Reddit activity following the group's second public video release to a paid/bot campaign, without identifying a sponsor; states independent verification would be possible but costly. (3 / 1:59:05–1:59:52)

**S6 — "Secret physics" as the disclosure thesis.** Central to the guest's own framing: aerospace contractors (Lockheed named explicitly) have held physics/materials knowledge since "at least the end of World War II"; disclosure, in his view, means releasing the *science*, not the hardware, precisely so an individual engineer stops being suspected personally of causing the UAP phenomenon. Presented as the guest's own theory, not sourced to any official. (3 / 0:47:22–0:50:48)

**S7 — AEC-CIA continuity via McCone.** John McCone chaired the Atomic Energy Commission immediately before becoming CIA Director in late 1961; the JFK-episode guest treats this personnel continuity as the reason McCone "definitely would have been privy to all of the secrets when it came to UFOs, given the nuclear connection" — an inference, not a sourced claim, but one that dovetails with the AEC-as-classification-instrument claim already logged independently in the Gerb corpus (S9 there).
