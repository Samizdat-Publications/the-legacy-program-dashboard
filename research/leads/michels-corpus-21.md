## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — custody, funding, classification mechanism

Numbered for citation. All are **the speaker's claims**, several explicitly relayed second-
or third-hand rather than sourced directly.

**S1 — Havana Syndrome/AHI as a deliberately contested intelligence question, not a settled
one.** Haseltine and Gilbert describe active, ongoing (as of taping) direct interviews with
AHI victims; Haseltine says there was "not unanimity" inside the intelligence community, with
one faction saying "it's real and we know what it is with very high likelihood" and an
official faction saying "nothing to see here." He frames the split as institutional rather
than evidentiary. (21-1 / 0:56:18-1:01:53)

**S2 — Sensor-capability concealment as an alternative to "hiding aliens."** Haseltine's own
inference, offered explicitly as such: government reluctance to release UAP sensor data may
reflect protecting "sources and methods" (i.e. concealing collection capability from
adversaries) rather than concealing non-human contact. (21-1 / 0:48:32-0:49:23)

**S3 — Round Table Foundation / Intelectron as a continuous, multi-decade, multi-agency-
funded parapsychology research pipeline that pre-dates and converges with SRI.** Per Milazzi:
Army, Navy, CIA and AEC money funded Puharich from ~1948 through the 1960s; by 1973-74 "Lab
Nine" was doing remote-viewing-style sessions with "space kids" contemporaneous with the
official SRI program's early years, and Milazzi cites a 1973 SRI-to-Lab-Nine document as
direct institutional linkage, disputed on tape by Hal Puthoff himself when confronted.
(21-2 / 2:04:44-2:07:24)

**S4 — MK-Ultra-style implantation as Milazzi's own working explanation for "the nine."**
Stated as his considered conclusion, not a hedge: when asked point-blank whether the channel-
led entities were real non-human contact or an implanted/manipulated belief, Milazzi answers
"the evidence would point to the latter." (21-2 / 2:10:16)

**S5 — Nuclear/AEC custody of a hearing-transmission ("microwave hearing") research thread,

project's corpus.** AEC funding for a device capable of covertly transmitting audio signals

Pandora research into Soviet radio-frequency weapons. (21-2 / 1:23:11-1:24:06)

**S6 — Whistleblower manuscript-as-discovery-tool.** Barber's stated technique: submit
material suspected to be classified to DOPSR/"Doppster" pre-publication review in small
chunks, and read the pattern of redactions as confirmation of which specific claims are
true. Framed explicitly by the speaker as turning the government's own review process into
"a Trojan horse." (21-4 / 0:16:50-0:17:42)

**S7 — A parallel command structure for crash-retrieval operations, run through private

his employer as "an unnamed aerospace giant," his operations as needing to be untraceable to
