**Status of everything below: UNVERIFIED LEAD MATERIAL.** Every line records *what a
named speaker claims*, attributed, with a file + timestamp pointer. Nothing here is

the wording matters the pointer is given instead so the claim can be re-read at source.

**Auto-transcript warning applies to every proper noun.** These are machine captions
(faster-whisper) with proper-noun corruption. Names below are marked:

- **[CONF]** — spelled consistently and matches a well-attested public figure,
  organisation, document or programme.
- **[PHON?]** — phonetically corrupted; my best reconstruction is given with the raw
  caption form in `backticks`. **Treat as a search string, not a fact.**

Timestamps are the marker nearest the statement, format `H:MM:SS`, matching the
transcript's own `**[H:MM:SS]**` blocks.

## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. Both are the **speaker's own claims**, attributed
to Grusch's testimony rather than sourced by Michels to a document.

**S1 — A competitive, cross-national reverse-engineering effort.** Michels states that
one detail of Grusch's 2023 testimony was "frequently overshadowed" by the headlines
about recovered bodies and craft: that there is an ongoing effort to reverse-engineer
recovered non-human technology, run as a competition against China and Russia to
"make the first modern super weapons." He restates this later in the same episode as
his own framing device rather than a new claim. No document, contract or office is
named as the vehicle for this competition — it is recorded here as vague. (03 /
0:03:30, 0:45:04)

**S2 — Rank inconsistency on a named claimed witness.** George Hoover is introduced as
a Navy Commander in one part of file 01 and referred to as "General" in another, for
what is presented as the same person and the same claim (Roswell crash occupants were
time-travelling humans). Flagged as a corruption tell rather than resolved either way.
(01 / 0:02:39, 0:29:10)

## 4. WHAT IS *NOT* IN THIS SLICE

- **File 02 (Tero Isokauppila, mushrooms)** contains no structural content under the
  brief's definition. It is a panspermia/mycology speculation piece — fungal
  intelligence, psilocybin and consciousness, the Santa Claus/Sami/Amanita muscaria
  thread — explicitly the kind of material (folklore, psychedelics) the brief says not
  to record. No government office, contractor, programme, document or named witness
  tied to the recovery/reverse-engineering programme appears anywhere in the file.
- **File 04 (Tom O'Neill, Manson)** contains no structural content under the brief's
  definition either, despite heavy CIA content. It concerns MKUltra, Operation Midnight
  Climax, COINTELPRO/CHAOS, Sydney Gottlieb, Dr. Jolly West and Jack Ruby — a real but
  entirely separate alleged CIA programme (domestic mind control, 1950s-70s) with no
  stated connection anywhere in the file to non-human craft, their recovery, or their
  custody. Recorded as a deliberate exclusion rather than an oversight.
- **File 03 (Pillsbury/Rogin, China)** is otherwise US-China geopolitics — TikTok,
  the United Front, Hunter Biden, Neil Bush, Elaine Chao, Wendy Deng Murdoch, the
  Thousand Talents programme, Charles Lieber, NSC-68, the Wuhan Institute of Virology —
  none of which ties to the mapped programme. Only the two Grusch mentions in §1b/§3
  qualify.
- **File 01** raises, but the speaker does not assert, a claim worth noting as a
  non-finding: no government office, SAP, or custody structure is named anywhere in
  the episode. Its structural content is limited to the named witnesses and the one
  programme (Stargate) in §1.
