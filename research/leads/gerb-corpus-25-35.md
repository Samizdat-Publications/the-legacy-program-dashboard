   else claiming*, with a file + timestamp pointer. Grading is the board's job, not this file's.
2. **Every proper noun is a LEAD, not a fact.** These are auto-generated captions with heavy
   proper-noun corruption. Where a name is phonetically uncertain I give `RAW → best guess` and
   say so. A prior session found `Holocron Engineering` was really **HoloChron LLC**; that exact
   corruption appears again in this slice (file 33 @ 00;57;11).
3. **No quotations longer than a short phrase.** Cite file + timestamp, not text.

   as vagueness.
