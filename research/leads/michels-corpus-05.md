## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, custody, funding, oversight, disclosure

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are **the speaker's claims**, several explicitly
labelled by the speaker as their own inference.

**S1 — A civilian contractor runs the "off-world" showing at a Navy facility.** Anderson's
account, filtered through Michels/Gerb's inference: instructors at NSWC Crane were "civilian
contracted companies... possibly SAIC," with SAIC also named as having "done some contracting
work with Crane in the past." Anderson never independently confirms the SAIC identification —
it is presented as Gerb's research. (01 / 43;02)

**S2 — A witness is shown classified technology and never followed up with.** Anderson: after
the sphere/gauntlet showing, he and the other Green Beret were told not to discuss it, signed
no additional paperwork beyond standard admin NDAs, and were never re-contacted or debriefed
again. He was later told (by unnamed instructors, per his account) that a person had been
harmed or killed retrieving the second item. (01 / 58;48)

**S3 — Nuclear-adjacent personnel selectively omit what they saw to preserve clearance and
career.** Woods states explicitly he did not report seeing the four "beings" out of fear of
losing his PRP clearance, his job, and never working the missile field again — he told the
full sighting/technical story but omitted that one detail, in writing, under his commander. (02
/ 59;33–1:00;25)

**S4 — A known Air Force disinformation specialist appears inside a routine nuclear-site
debrief eight years before his most famous operation.** Doty is placed at Woods's 1977
Ellsworth medical debrief, reporting to Capt. Jack Reed, OSI; Woods says Doty's stated
after-the-fact reason for being there was to collect his and Michael Johnson's medical
records. Michels frames this as pre-dating Doty's Paul Bennewitz disinformation campaign by
roughly eight years. (02 / 54;40–55;30, 1:09;52)

**S5 — A partner disappears from all institutional record.** Woods says Michael Johnson has
been unfindable in hospital records, TWS (Together We Serve) military-social records, and by
"every resource, including... law-enforcement officers," for decades; no surname is
recoverable. Woods explicitly connects this to a broader pattern: Terry Lovelace's camping
companion "Toby" is similarly gone. (02 / 55;30–56;06, 2:03;19)

**S6 — Program-size and workforce figures are treated as an evidentiary base by researchers
without independent verification.** Robert Hastings's 167-witness figure (cited across files
01, 02 and 04) is repeatedly re-quoted, revised in dialogue by Michels ("it was only like 12
