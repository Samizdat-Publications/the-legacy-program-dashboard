## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them if useful. All are the named speaker's claims, several
explicitly flagged by Mishlove himself as unverified or as his own inference.

**S1 — UAP disclosure as a controlled psyop culminating in a staged event (293, guest's own
claim).** Bart Sibrel: existing and forthcoming government "leaks" that UFOs/aliens are real
are being managed to build public credulity toward a future faked alien invasion, and he
attributes the seed of this specifically to a Wernher von Braun deathbed warning relayed
through an unnamed "closest friend". He extends this into a broader claim that the same
government that (per his separate, out-of-scope thesis) faked the moon landing is now capable
of, and intends, a much larger deception. No source document, name or date given for the von
Braun warning; record the vagueness. (293, 1:44:00–1:45:38)

**S2 — The SRI remote-viewing programme's funding logic (294, Mishlove relaying Puthoff/Targ
and David Morehouse).** Explicit cost-benefit reasoning attributed to an unnamed general or
intelligence official who told remote viewer David Morehouse: even a 1% success rate was
"worth a lot of money", especially during the Cold War. Total spend across the programme's
life is put by Mishlove at roughly $20 million over 20 years — his own figure, offered
without a document citation, as evidence the parapsychology side of the programme was tiny
next to the whole military/intelligence research budget. (294, 0:21:35–0:22:23; 1:10:55)

**S3 — SRI's need for a low profile drove the CIA relationship into the open, briefly (294,
Mishlove's own account).** Puthoff and Targ told Mishlove directly, in 1976, that they
"couldn't have" the erratic and publicity-seeking Ted Owens near the programme because "we're
working with the CIA, we're getting government funding. We need to keep a very low profile" —
and handed their file on Owens to Mishlove, then a graduate student, to get it off their own
hands. This is Mishlove's direct, first-person account of being told this, not a secondhand
claim. (294, 0:47:37)

**S4 — Deliberate disinformation using civilian UFO witnesses as unwitting conduits (294,
Mishlove relaying Jacobson on the Bennewitz case).** Doty allegedly encouraged Bennewitz's
belief his sightings were extraterrestrial specifically to plant that narrative inside the
wider UFO research community, on the theory (Mishlove's own gloss, presented as likely
correct) that the real subject being protected was classified aircraft testing and that
Soviet-aligned observers inside the UFO community were the actual intended audience for the
deception. (294, 1:49:02–1:50:51)

**S5 — The same tradecraft applied institutionally: gorilla masks at Area 51 (294, Mishlove,
unsourced anecdote).** Test pilots for early jet aircraft allegedly carried gorilla masks to
wear if a civilian aircraft came into view, so that any resulting sighting report would read
as an obvious hoax rather than trigger investigation of a real classified aircraft. No
document or date given; presented as a well-known story rather than something Mishlove
personally verified. (294, 1:53:29)

**S6 — Bigelow's UAP data-collection arrangement ran through his private company, not a
federal channel (294, Mishlove's own account, corroborating Danny Jones's independent
framing).** Danny Jones states his understanding that an FAA-adjacent reporting norm exists
whereby pilots seeing UAP report to a private aerospace company (implicitly Bigelow
Aerospace) rather than to the FAA itself. Mishlove confirms the broad shape: Bigelow, via his
personal relationship with then-Senate Majority Leader Harry Reid, set up BAASS/AAWSAP
(both PHON-reconstructed, see §1a) to collect and assess UAP reports as a national-security
and aviation-safety question, reaching out to air-traffic-control and aerospace personnel for
reports because "nobody was making a systematic effort to collect it" outside organisations
like MUFON. (294, 1:44:24–1:47:09)

**S7 — A senior intelligence-community figure (NRO) allegedly maintained a personal
relationship with a private citizen claiming psychokinetic/UFO-summoning powers (294,
Mishlove, secondhand).** Mishlove states Owens was visited by people from the military, NASA
and "other CIA agencies", and specifically "some really shadowy guy in the NRO" who attended
Owens's children's football games and invited the family to NASA launches at Cape Canaveral.
No name given for the NRO individual. (294, 1:52:37–1:53:29)

## 4. CROSS-EPISODE NOTE

**The same Robert Bigelow programme surfaces independently in two files of this batch.** File
289 (off-subject DMT episode) has Danny Jones and Danny Goler discussing, unprompted and with
no connection to file 294's guest, a grant Robert Bigelow funded for research into near-death
experiences — misremembering the researcher's name as "John Bigelow" before correcting to
Robert Bigelow, and linking it to a prior guest's (Jeffrey Long's) NDE research (289,
3:16:00–3:18:40). File 294's guest, Jeffrey Mishlove, is a direct participant in the same
underlying programme: he won Bigelow's $500,000 "best evidence for survival of consciousness"
essay contest and sat on the board of the resulting Bigelow Institute for Consciousness
Studies (294, 1:30:03–1:43:31). Both point at the same real, named, checkable programme from
two unconnected conversations recorded three weeks apart — worth flagging as convergent, not
as independent corroboration of any claim *within* the programme (see this board's CLAUDE.md
§2 on why two agents/episodes agreeing is not itself corroboration).
