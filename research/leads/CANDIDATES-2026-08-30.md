## C. The sharpest single finding

**Special Report No. 14 does not name Battelle anywhere on its own face.** A full
OCR search found no occurrence of Battelle, Columbus, Stork, contractor, "prepared
by" or 19741. The attribution rests **entirely** on the Project Stork status
reports. That *strengthens* the board's argument rather than weakening it, and the
`project-stork` node should say so in exactly those terms.
