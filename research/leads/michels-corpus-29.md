## 3. WHAT THE STRUCTURAL FILE ACTUALLY CARRIES

File 04 is the only file in this batch that touches the board's subject, and even there the
content is thin and mostly historical scene-setting rather than new custody/oversight claims.
The through-line as the narration and Loeb present it:

**1947–1952, the "open-mindedness" window.** Kenneth Arnold's sighting and the Roswell/Trinity
references (the narration's own phrase is "known crashes like those at Trinity and Roswell,"
0:05:40 — Trinity is the 1945 nuclear test site, not a reported UFO crash site, and its
pairing with Roswell here looks like a narration error, not a transcription corruption; flag
and do not carry forward as a second crash site) mark the start of a period the narration
calls comparatively open. Operation Blue Book was stood up in this window.

**July 1952, the Washington DC flap.** Presented as the hinge event: it produces the CIA memo
(D3) and, from that, the Robertson Panel, which Michels' narration credits with the pivot to
"systematically downplay the phenomena." This is structurally the same shape as the Robertson
Panel material already on the board from the Gerb corpus (see `gerb-corpus-01-12.md` §1a, §3)
— nothing here adds a new fact to it, but it is an independent restatement from a different
speaker and source chain (Michels' own narration rather than Gerb's).

**1968, Condon closes Blue Book.** No new detail beyond what the wider board already carries.

**2007–2017, AATIP and its disclosure.** The narration's claim that AATIP was "run under the
auspices of" Bigelow Aerospace, sourced to Leslie Kean's 2017 NYT reporting, matches the
public record in substance; the caption corrupts "Bigelow Aerospace" as "Big Low airspace,"
which is recorded as a PHON? item above rather than silently corrected.

**Present day, the Galileo Project as a stated response to government custody of data.** Loeb
frames the whole project explicitly as independent of government custody: "they own all the
data, all the footage, all the materials, and it's up to them as to whether they want to
disclose that to the public" is the narration's framing of the problem (0:03:00), and Loeb's
own statement of the Galileo Project's purpose is to "get open data and analyze it in a
transparent way" (0:12:39) precisely because government sensor data is "military grade"
instrumentation "not the same quality ... as a scientific expedition" and may be classified
for reasons of not revealing "the quality of the instruments being used" to adversaries
(0:11:44–0:12:39). This is Loeb's own inference, not a sourced claim about a specific
programme, office or document — record it as his stated reasoning, not as a new structural
fact about the programme this board maps.
