`research/leads/michels-absent/group-1.tsv` (180 ABSENT names, batches 01 to 05).

node records for the nodes named in section C. Every node id below was confirmed against
that file. Section D's first group reports a cross-check defect rather than noise, and it
is listed first for that reason.
