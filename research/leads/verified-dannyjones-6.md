### What the documents establish

**The 1988 report.** It answers section 1133 of Public Law 100-180 and is "an unclassified
summary of a classified report that we sent to you on April 1, 1988." Its finding: "the
Department of Defense does not adequately follow criteria to be used in determining the need to
establish programs as special access required." It states who may create one: "Only the three
service secretaries and Deputy Under Secretary of Defense for Policy are authorized to establish
special access programs." It states the accounting failure the board's oversight view argues
from: "The Deputy Under Secretary of Defense for Policy is required to maintain a listing of all
special access programs, but does not do so because of security concerns." On the size criterion
it records that Air Force accesses "were in the tens of thousands" and that an OSD official said
"small is relative". And it carries the Army sentence quoted in finding 1 above.

**The 1989 report.** It answers Boxer's request of 12 March 1987 and summarises a classified
report of 13 March 1989. It gives the taxonomy: "There are three broad categories of special
access programs: acquisition, intelligence, and military operations." It gives the vernacular
and its status: the programmes "are commonly referred to as 'black' programs, although this term
is not used in DOD policy." It names examples DoD had acknowledged: the B-2 Advanced Technology
Bomber, the Advanced Tactical Aircraft and the Advanced Cruise Missile. It extends the carve-out
figure the board's `gao-130581` node argues from: "According to the Service, the number of
'carve-out' contracts has grown from 63 in 1971 to 1,061 in 1987", and "The Service assumed full
security inspection duties for the Army in 1987." It also records that the number of DoD audit
and inspection agencies reviewing SAPs "has increased from two to eight since 1978."

**The INSCOM histories.** The FY 1981 review carries a GRILL FLAME section at its pages 89 to 90,
partly withheld under 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(1) and (b)(3). What is released says: "Project GRILL FLAME
originated as a specially controlled and compartmented activity under the direct supervision of
the ACSI, DA, and in coordination with other DOD agencies concerned with related activity. ACSI
orally tasked HQ INSCOM to evaluate Project GRILL FLAME in October 1978." Then the transfer
sentence quoted in finding 6. Its source note names "IA(HQ) Form 32, Formalization of Project
GRILL FLAME (10 Sep 81) (S/NOFORN)."
