## B. The TR-3B is the next hub node, and it is the same shape as the ARV

The ARV build established the pattern the 18 September handoff section 3b asked for: the
thing claimed about is a node, it is the hub, and every claimant runs INTO it. `arv-fluxliner`
is the first craft node this board has ever had.

**The TR-3B is the same case and a bigger one.** It is one of the most widely repeated craft
claims in the whole subject, it traces to a single named claimant (Fouche, 1998), and the
board carries nothing for it. A `tr-3b` hub with Fouche as its originating claimant, plus the
debunking literature as `denial` edges, is a clean second application of the rule.

**Before any of that is built, the honest work is:** open Fouche's 1998 presentation and the
"military documents" the site links, establish whether the documents are what they are said
to be, and find the sceptical literature. The ARV dossier's discipline applies: the claim's
anchor is the dated artefact of the claim, never proof of the thing claimed.

## C. What NOT to take from this site

The site's research page is a list of people its anonymous owner rates, written in
promotional prose ("one of the most credible voices"). That is the site's opinion and it is
not a finding. The names are useful as a CHECKLIST against the board. The characterisations
are not sources and must not become node descriptions.

The Office of Global Access passage is quoted from DailyMail reporting of anonymous sources.
If OGA is built, it is built from Richelson or from an openable record, not from that page.
