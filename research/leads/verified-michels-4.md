## 17. Monica (Jacinto) Reza

**VERDICT: PARTLY CONFIRMED.** The missing-person record exists as a state record and was
opened. One published patent application in her maiden name was opened and it does name the
two comparator alloys the transcript claims. The Mondaloy attribution, the AFRL co-funding
and the plural "patents" are not in it.

### The California Department of Justice record

`https://oag.ca.gov/missing/person/monica-jacinto-reza` is the California Attorney General's
public missing-person record. It gives the name **Monica Jacinto Reza**, the alias **Monica
Andrea Jacinto**, date of last contact **22 June 2025**, missing from **Los Angeles County**,
investigating agency **Los Angeles County Sheriff's Office**, agency case number
**025-00905-1257-400**, and a clothing description of "Red long sleeve, green hiking pants,
and hiking shoes". The record carries no narrative of the circumstances and no location
inside the county, so **the transcript's "Angeles National Forest" is not in the state
record**. The date, 22 June 2025, is.

### The congressional letters

On **20 April 2026** Oversight Chairman James Comer and Subcommittee Chairman Eric Burlison
sent effectively identical letters to NASA, the FBI, the Department of War and the
Department of Energy. Two were read in full.

| URL | What is at it |
|---|---|
| https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/NASA-Missing-Scientists-Letter_4.20.26.pdf | Two-page letter to Administrator Jared Isaacman, 20 April 2026, signed by Comer and Burlison, copied to Ranking Members Garcia and Frost. |
| https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/FBI-Missing-Scientists-Letter_4.20.26.pdf | Two-page letter to Director Kash Patel, same date, same body but for the addressee and one clause. |
| https://burlison.house.gov/media/press-releases/comer-burlison-seek-information-missing-nuclear-and-rocket-scientists | Press release of the same date carrying links to all four letters. |

The letters name her: "Another JPL alum, Monica Reza, who served as the director of the NASA
Lab's Materials Processing Group, disappeared while hiking in California in June 2025 and
remains missing." They also carry the McCasland link, reporting that accounts "have even
alleged a direct link between Ms. Reza and General McCasland, characterizing them as having a
'close professional connection' through an Air Force-funded research program in the early
2000s".

**Three corrections the letters force on the board's existing node.**

| `missing-scientists-probe-2026` says | The letters say |
|---|---|
| "eleven people" | "at least ten individuals" |
| "have died or vanished under unexplained circumstances since 2022" | the string "began in 2023 with the death of Michael David Hicks" |
| sourced to one Fox News story, every claim Tier 2 | four signed committee letters, published, Tier 1 as to what the Committee asked |

**And a fourth thing, which is the important one.** The Committee frames its own subject as
"recent unconfirmed public reporting" and footnotes every factual assertion in the letter to
the Daily Mail of 11 April 2026 and to New York Post pieces of 9 April, 18 March and
21 March 2026. The letter does not adopt those reports. It asks four agencies whether they
are true. **Nothing about Reza's employment, about the McCasland connection, or about the
count of affected people is established by the letter.** It establishes that the Committee
asked, and it establishes the deadline it set, 27 April 2026.

### The patent

`https://patents.google.com/patent/US20100266442A1/en` is **US 2010/0266442 A1,
"Burn-resistant and high tensile strength metal alloys"**, application 12/626,205, filed
25 November 2009, published 21 October 2010, inventors **Monica A. Jacinto and Dallis Ann
Hardwick**, no assignee of record. It is a continuation of application 10/769,195 of
30 January 2004, itself a continuation-in-part of 09/954,835 of 18 September 2001. **All
three US applications are abandoned**, the file showing abandonment on 1 December 2012 after
an examiner's answer or Board decision. A Russian application, RU2002124799/02A of
17 September 2002, sits in the same family.

| Transcript claim | What the document says |
|---|---|
| "her Mondaloy patents", plural and granted | one published application family, three abandoned US filings, no granted US patent. The word Mondaloy does not appear. A separate search of `patents.google.com` for "mondaloy" returns two patents, both Florida Turbine Technologies turbopump filings by Timothy J. Miller, neither hers. |
| names Haynes 214 and Monel K500 as comparators | **CONFIRMED.** The specification discusses "Haynes 214" as a nickel-chromium-aluminium-iron high-temperature oxidation-resistant alloy and "Monel Alloy K-500" as a nickel-copper-aluminium alloy, alongside Inconel MA 754 and Waspaloy. |
| AFRL co-funding of the alloy from 1999 | **NOT IN THE DOCUMENT.** There is no statement regarding federally sponsored research, no government contract number, and no mention of the Air Force Research Laboratory anywhere in it. |

**The identification is an inference and must be graded as one.** The state record gives the
alias "Monica Andrea Jacinto". The patent inventor is "Monica A. Jacinto". The subject
matter, a burn-resistant high-strength nickel alloy for oxygen-rich rocket-engine service,
matches the materials-processing role the Committee letter reports. That is a strong match on
name, initial and specialty. It is not a documented identity and cannot be carried at Tier 1.

**Tier: T1** for every field of the California Department of Justice record. **T1** for the
existence, dates, inventors, status and comparator alloys of the patent application. **T1**
for what the Committee letters ask and how they characterise their own sourcing. **T2** for
anything the letters relay from the Daily Mail or the Post. **T3** for the identification of
the inventor with the missing person.

**BOARD RECOMMENDATION: yes, a NEW NODE**, built from the state record and the letters, with
the patent attached at Tier 1 and the identification stated as Tier 3 in its own claim text.
Do not carry the Mondaloy name, the AFRL funding, the plural "patents", or the McCasland
"close professional connection" as anything other than what they are.

## 18. GEC Marconi engineer deaths

**VERDICT: PARTLY CONFIRMED, with two elements of the transcript CONTRADICTED.** Parliament
addressed the deaths repeatedly between March 1987 and April 1988, the coroners' verdicts on
two of the men are stated in a minister's own answer, and a Ministry of Defence police fraud
investigation into Marconi contracts is separately documented. The 1982 to 1990 span, the
"joint US/UK SDI programme" framing, and the description of all the men as Marconi engineers
are not supported.

Six items were opened through the Hansard API at `hansard-api.parliament.uk`, which returns
the same official-report text as `hansard.parliament.uk` and reaches where the site's own
search page does not (that page answers a direct request with an HTTP 403 interstitial).

| Date | Section | Who | What it says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 Mar 1987 | Deaths (Bristol), Commons written answers | Campbell-Savours asking; **Douglas Hogg** answering | Avon and Somerset police have completed their inquiries. "The coroner recorded an open verdict in the case of Mr. Dajibhai and a verdict of suicide in the case of Mr. Sharif." Derbyshire's investigation into Singh-Gida's disappearance continues. Other than that the two men "were employed in different parts of the Marconi company" and that Dajibhai and Singh-Gida probably knew each other at Loughborough in 1979, "there is at present no evidence of to link the three cases". |
| 23 Mar 1987 | Mr Avtar Singh-Gida | Campbell-Savours; **Archie Hamilton** answering | Singh-Gida "was engaged on an unclassified research agreement for the MOD at Loughborough university on matters unrelated to the strategic defense initiative studies." |
| 25 Mar 1987 | Mr V Dajibhai And Mr A Sharif | Cartwright | Asks for publication of the Avon and Somerset reports into "the death in August 1986 of Vimal Dajibhai and the death in October 1986 of Ashad Sharif". |
| 9 Apr 1987 | Scientists (Deaths) | Deakins; **Douglas Hogg** answering | Repeats the two verdicts and states the investigation was run by "divisional CID officers under the supervision of a senior CID officer at Headquarters". |
| 31 Mar 1988 | Scientists (Deaths) | **Doug Hoyle** asking; **Tim Sainsbury** answering | Hoyle asks three questions: for an inquiry into the security implications of the deaths of Victor Moore, Ashad Sharif, Vimal Dajibhai, David Sands, Peter Peapell and Dr John Brittan; whether Brittan or Peapell did subcontracting work for Marconi while employed by the department; and whether the Ministry of Defence police investigation into Marconi contracts had ever questioned or examined any of them. Sainsbury: "The answer to all three questions is no." |
| 19 Apr 1988 | Mr Peter Peapell, and Inquests (Police Investigations) | Hoyle; **Sainsbury** and **Hogg** answering | "Mr. Peter Peapell, was a lecturer at the Royal Military college of science, Shrivenham." On the inquests of Dajibhai, Peapell and Sands, "each of these cases was fully investigated by the police both before and after the inquests." |

Separately, the Marconi investigation the third Hoyle question refers to is itself on the
record. On **2 November 1987** Sainsbury stated that Ministry of Defence police
investigations "into allegations of irregularities over contracts with Marconi Defence
Systems began in October 1986 and are continuing", and that direction and prosecution had
been referred to the Director of Public Prosecutions' fraud investigation group on
19 August 1987.

**What the transcript claimed that this does not establish.**

| Transcript (`michels-triage-4.md` A2) | The record |
|---|---|
| deaths spanning 1982 to 1990 | Parliament addresses deaths from **August 1986** to early **1988**. No question or answer opened here reaches 1982 or 1990. |
| "on a joint US/UK SDI programme" | **CONTRADICTED** for the one man an SDI link was actually put to a minister about. Hamilton stated Singh-Gida's Ministry of Defence agreement was "unrelated to the strategic defense initiative studies". No answer connects any of the others to SDI. |
| GEC Marconi engineers | Only Dajibhai and Sharif are placed inside Marconi by a minister, and in "different parts" of it. Peapell was a Royal Military College of Science lecturer. Sainsbury answered "no" to whether Brittan or Peapell subcontracted for Marconi. |
| "closed, inquested and citable" | Half right. Verdicts are stated for Dajibhai (open) and Sharif (suicide), and inquests are confirmed to have been held for Dajibhai, Peapell and Sands. **The inquest files themselves were not opened.** UK coroners' files are not published, nothing was found online, and nothing is asserted about them here. |

One oddity worth carrying forward rather than smoothing: Hoyle's third question names
"Dr. John Knight" where his first two name "Dr. John Brittan". Both appear in the same
published answer. That is Hansard's own text, not a slip introduced here.

**Tier: T1** for every quotation above, each being an entry in the official report of a House
of Parliament. **NOT GRADEABLE** for the inquest files, the 1982 to 1990 span, and any count
of deaths beyond the seven names Parliament used.

**BOARD RECOMMENDATION: yes, a NEW NODE**, and it is the most useful of the five. This board
argues about people who die or vanish near classified defence work. Here is the same shape,
in another country, forty years earlier, with a national legislature asking the questions and
a defence ministry answering on the record. The answer being "no" three times over is exactly
the kind of documented denial the board's denial machinery exists to grade.
