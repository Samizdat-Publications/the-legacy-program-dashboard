## 2. Future Imagery Architecture

**VERDICT: PARTLY CONFIRMED.** The programme exists in a Senate intelligence committee's
own published account of its oversight work, which is exactly the class of record the
triage row wanted. What that record does not contain is the failure: no cancellation, no
dollar figure, no contractor.

### What was opened

| URL | What is at it |
|---|---|
| https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CRPT-109srpt360/html/CRPT-109srpt360.htm | Senate Report 109-360, "Committee Activities" of the Select Committee on Intelligence, 109th Congress 2d Session, ordered to be printed 16 November 2006, covering the 108th Congress |
| https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-IC21/html/GPO-IC21-6.html | IC21: The Intelligence Community in the 21st Century, chapter VI, "IMINT: Imagery Intelligence", House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence staff study |

### What the document establishes

Under "E. AUDITS", S. Rept. 109-360 describes the Committee's Audit and Investigations
Staff, created in 1988 "to provide 'a credible independent arm for committee review of
covert action programs and other specific IC functions and issues'", and lists five
in-depth reviews completed during the 108th Congress. The first is headed "1. The NRO's
Future Imagery Architecture Program", and reads: "During 2003, the Committee's Audit and
Investigations Staff examined the acquisition management processes for the National
Reconnaissance Office's (NRO) Future Imagery Architecture (FIA) program. The audit focused
on the NRO's contract administration and financial management mechanisms as they related
to FIA, in addition to a general overview of the FIA program. The National
Geospatial-Intelligence Agency's segment of the FIA program also was examined."

That establishes four things at Tier 1: FIA was an NRO acquisition programme; it was
audited by the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence's own auditors in 2003; the audit
was aimed at contract administration and financial management rather than at capability;
and NGA held a segment of it.

### What the transcript claimed that this document does NOT establish

The claim relayed from Dwayne Day (batch 26 file 12, 0:14:00 to 0:14:53) is that FIA was
the KH-11's planned successor, that it was cancelled, and that NROL-49 was assembled from
spare parts as it collapsed. **The opened report says none of that.** It does not use the
words cancelled, terminated or restructured, names no contractor, gives no cost and does
not mention KH-11 or NROL-49. The report's date, 16 November 2006, is later than the events
the transcript describes, and the report still speaks of FIA as a programme under audit.

**What was tried for the failure record and did not answer.** Four further Senate
intelligence committee documents were fetched in full and searched: CRPT-110srpt5 (FY2007
Intelligence Authorization Act), CRPT-110srpt57 (Committee Activities, January 2005 to
December 2006, the period in which the transcript places the collapse), CRPT-110srpt75
(FY2008 Intelligence Authorization Act) and CRPT-111srpt6 (Committee Activities, January
2007 to January 2009). **Zero occurrences of "Future Imagery" in any of the four.**
GPO-IC21-6 also has zero. The govinfo search API answered OVER_RATE_LIMIT on every
attempt, so a full-text sweep was not available, and `gao.gov` was not reached this
session. The cancellation, the dollar scale and the prime contract remain NOT OPENED.
