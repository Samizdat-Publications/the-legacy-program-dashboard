## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the named speaker's claims.

**S1 -- Strategic deception has two distinct forms, per Jacobsen's CIA sources: cover and misinformation.** Cover is a plausible alternate explanation (Area 51 press told the 1955 U-2-crash fatalities were "secret atomic bomb" scientists). Misinformation is deliberately incorrect information fed into a target audience's own belief system -- illustrated by CIA test pilots keeping gorilla masks in the cockpit so that any civilian sighting report would not be believed. (228 / 1:15:45–1:20:00)

**S2 -- "Need to know" as compartmentalisation, illustrated at Area 51.** Jacobsen: one room works on the engine, another on the windshield wipers, and neither knows what the whole aircraft is for -- the same principle she says applies to the nuclear command-and-control "system of systems." (228 / 0:53:09–0:54:04)
