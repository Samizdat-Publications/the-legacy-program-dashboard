## 3. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS — compartmentalisation, deception, custody, oversight

Numbered so the board can cite them. All are the **named speaker's claims**, several explicitly
their own inference.

**S1 — Cover-story protocol runs both directions.** Doty, describing his own working rule as
an AFOSI case officer: when a witness saw real classified hardware, agents let them believe
it was a UFO (the Bennewitz method); when a witness saw something AFOSI itself could not
explain or attribute, and the witness already held a clearance for adjacent work, they were
read in or made to sign an NDA rather than told a cover story, "because he wouldn't have
believed it." Doty states this was case-by-case, not a fixed script. (2 / 0:29:42–1:00:29)

**S2 — Deception operations are authored at headquarters, not in the field.** The Bennewitz
operational plan was drafted at AFOSI HQ after a case-agent report went up through channels
(the secure "KY3" phone line named specifically); the field officer executed a plan he did not
write. Doty extends this explicitly to the 1988 "UFO Cover-Up Live" TV special, which he says
was recorded 15 months before air as part of a joint OSI/FBI operation "to draw somebody out"
who had disclosed classified information — Doty had a script and, by his own account, did not
know at the time which parts were true. (2 / 0:26:57–1:41:09)

**S3 — Civilian UFO organisations are treated as a counterintelligence collection channel in
both directions.** Per Doty: Soviet-aligned "influencers" penetrated MUFON, APRO and NICAP
because civilian field investigators sometimes obtained sighting reports the military never
received; AFOSI ran its own assets inside the same groups (Bill Moore named specifically) both
to monitor foreign penetration and, later, to seed disinformation outward. (2 / 1:07:06–1:12:10)

**S4 — A parallel, unacknowledged abduction-investigation function inside DIA predates
AFOSI's UFO briefing track.** Doty says he was not briefed into "9Q" until 1979, but that two
DIA agents visited him in 1980 identifying themselves as belonging to a DIA branch that
"investigates abductions" and had existed "for years" — which he read, after the fact, as
confirmation that DIA had taken the underlying phenomenon seriously well before his own
access began. (2 / 0:23:18–0:25:06)

**S5 — Access is stratified into full, partial and "access-only" briefings**, the last
triggered by an on-call incident (a coded phone alert) rather than a standing clearance. Doty
estimates roughly 20 full briefings across his career, an unspecified number of partial
briefings (helping another agent on need-to-know), and access-only exposure to specific
incidents (crash sites, the Carthusian-monk transport, a "visitor" facility he saw by
accident and had to self-report on an internal form). (2 / 0:47:02–0:48:47, 3:09:19–3:10:13)

**S6 — Different alleged non-human "species" are treated as operationally distinct, with
different countermeasures developed per type.** Doty states flatly that no single
countermeasure works against more than one kind, and that the technique used to interdict the
sergeant's abductions "is only going to work on that particular extraterrestrial." He declines
to describe the technology beyond calling it "not guns... a frequency." (2 / 0:22:26,
2:44:37–2:45:33)

**S7 — Contractors as deniability layer.** Doty, asked whether DIA still controls the
Stargate/SRI-descended remote-viewing work: "I think that it's being still controlled by DIA,
but I feel that it's been contracted out... to remove yourself for any plausible deniability
that the government might want." (2 / 0:25:06–0:26:04)

**S8 — Evidentiary corroboration built from an abduction case becomes reusable protocol.**
Doty says techniques and physical "traits" documented from the sergeant's case were used
afterward by the intelligence community both to assess the credibility of other abduction
claims and to build a targeted, species-specific protective/interdiction method — developed
"during her time" and refined afterward, by "the intelligence community and contractors."
(2 / 0:20:29–0:23:18)

**S9 — A gifted-testing programme with unexplained memory gaps, run by outside adults in
suits, working in pairs, not employed by the school.** Mossbridge's own account of "SOAR":
weekly private sessions with total memory loss inside the room, testing that was explicitly
not IQ or hearing testing despite being presented that way, and a FOIA request about the
programme that (per her account) coincided suspiciously with the progress of a federal-job
background check she was undergoing at the same time. She and her mother both speculate CIA
involvement; neither offers documentary evidence. Record as a lead only — this is the
weakest-sourced structural claim in the batch. (1 / 0:18:18–0:29:54)
