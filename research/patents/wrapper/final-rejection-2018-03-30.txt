3. Claims 1-4 rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(a) or 35 U.S.C. 112 (pre-AIA), first
paragraph, as failing to comply with the enablement requirement. ... Claim 1
states using "microwave emitters such that the microwave emitters create high
frequency electromagnetic waves through the resonant cavity causing the outer
resonant cavity wall to vibrate in an accelerated mode and create a local
polarized vacuum outside the outer resonant cavity wall". However, when
referring to the specification to ascertain more about the microwave emitters
needed in this system it is seen that for a high energy electromagnetic field to
polarize a quantum vacuum as claimed it would take 10^9 teslas and 10^18V/m. The
Examiner holds that these levels are not feasible with current technology so how
would someone of ordinary skill in the art be able to know how to create this
craft? The largest magnetic field ever created is 10^3 teslas and a neutron star
is 10^6 teslas so how are you using a microwave emitter that produces a magnetic
field that is three orders of magnitude greater than a neutron star? As far as
the electricity need a fusion reactor produces electricity on the order of 10^4
V/m so how are you claiming a craft that produces electricity that is fourteen
orders of magnitude greater than a fusion reactor? Since the amount of magnetic
field and electricity is far beyond the scope of what can be created, the
Examiner holds that this claiming of this microwave emitter is not enabled such
that any person skill in the art could make and use the inventions.

6. Response to Arguments

The declaration by Dr. James Sheehy under 37 CFR 1.132 filed 1/23/2018 is
insufficient to overcome the rejection of claims 1-4 based upon 35 USC 112(a)
and 35 USC 112(b) as set forth in the last Office action because:

  a. With respect to the first argument that lasers have evolved and become more
  powerful then what was originally present, this has no relevance to the current
  claim set. Just because a thought like a laser turned out to be correct and
  workable does not mean that every math based theory will turn out to be
  accurate and buildable. The Applicant has still not provided enablement as to
  how magnetic and electric fields that are not remotely even close to be produce
  will create an inertial mass reduction.

  b. With respect to the second argument that Dr. Pais is funded and working on
  experiments does not provide proof that magnetic fields on the order of 10^9
  Teslas and electric fields on the order of 10^18 V/m can be produced let alone
  that they can then be used to create a local polarized vacuum which results in
  an inertial mass reduction device.

  c. With respect to third argument that China is already investing significantly
  in this area, this has nothing to do with what is patentable. If anyone else
  filed this Application they would receive the same rejection.

7. Applicant's arguments filed 1/23/2018 have been fully considered but they are
not persuasive.

  d. With respect to the first argument on pages 3-4 that the concept of the
  Application has been published by peer reviewed papers, specifically AIAA
  2017-5343 and SAE 2017-01-2040. The Examiner holds that just because a concept
  was presented at an AIAA forum and that a SAE committee board, peer reviewed the
  concept does not equate to the device being patentable. The Applicant has still
  not provided how the system is enabled to be made and operated with the
  impossible electrical and magnetic fields required.

  e. With respect to the second argument on page 5, that "this demonstrates that
  the amount of magnetic field and electricity required to enable this invention
  can be created", the Examiner respectfully disagrees. Presenting math at a
  conference doesn't prove anything physical. It like saying that you can math out
  tachyons by playing with relativistic equations but that doesn't "prove" that
  they exist nor can they then be used to create a physical object that can be
  patented.

  f. With respect to the third argument on pages 5-6, that high frequency
  piezoelectrics are known, this does not equate to what is being claimed. ...

  g. With respect to the fourth argument on pages 6-8, the Applicant alleges "one
  must realize that the high energy/high frequency electromagnetic field generator
  responsible for the inertial mass diminution effect would generate a repulsive
  EM energy field while in earth's atmosphere, thereby repelling air molecules in
  its path of ascent/flight", however, this is not true. There is no such thing as
  a "repulsive EM energy field". Electromagnetism can be repulsive in certain
  circumstances, but there is no blanket field that repels everything. The rest of
  the arguments are to the claims and the AIAA presentation at a space forum which
  do not overcome the underlying issue that the Application is not enabled to
  someone of ordinary skill in the art to know how this could be made as it is
  impossible to generate the electric and magnetic field that would be required to
  create the so called claimed effect of inertial mass reduction.
