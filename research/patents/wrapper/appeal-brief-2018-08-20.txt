Office to show unpatentability and remains there even after a prima facie case.

THE EVIDENCE APPENDIX -- AND A CORRECTION WORTH CARRYING

There are NOT two Sheehy affidavits. There is ONE declaration under 37 C.F.R. 1.132,
signed 11 May 2018, filed TWICE:
  - 24 May 2018, with the after-final reply (image JHRQ6JRIRXEAPX0.pdf); and
  - 20 August 2018, re-filed at page 22 of 41 in this brief's Evidence Appendix
    (image JLZJWBOORXEAPX0.pdf).
The text is identical. Any account describing two distinct affidavits is wrong, and
this note exists because an earlier pass of this project made that error.

The declaration rests its enablement assertion on "the peer[] reviewed papers described
in the [I]nventor Amendment dated January 23, 2018". Those papers are also in the
bundle, and TWO OF THE FIVE ARE PAIS'S OWN CONFERENCE PAPERS:
  - Pais, "A Hybrid Craft using an Inertial Mass Modification Device", AIAA SPACE Forum,
    Orlando, 12-14 September 2017 (image JCTF8ZGUPXXIFW1.pdf)
  - Pais, "High Frequency Gravitational Waves - Induced Propulsion", SAE International
    2017-01-2040, published 19 September 2017 (image JCTF9VU6PXXIFW1.pdf)
  - three third-party technical papers: a J Electroceramics (2007) review of
    piezoelectric materials for high frequency medical imaging; IEEE Transactions on
    Microwave Theory and Techniques vol 51 no 1 (March 2003), Coakley et al, on
    estimating Q-factors and resonant frequencies; and a Laser Physics Letters paper on
    a nonequilibrium plasma channel in a gaseous medium.

This matters for how the cluster is presented. The corroboration Sheehy invokes is in
part the inventor citing himself, and a sceptical reader will find that in ten minutes.
Better stated here than discovered by them.
