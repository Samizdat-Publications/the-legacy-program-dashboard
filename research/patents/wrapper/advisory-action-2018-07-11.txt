THE REPLY FILED 24 May 2018 FAILS TO PLACE THIS APPLICATION IN CONDITION FOR
ALLOWANCE. NO NOTICE OF APPEAL FILED

STATUS OF CLAIMS
  Claim(s) rejected: 1-4

Continuation Sheet (PTOL-303), Application No. 15/141,270
Continuation of REQUEST FOR RECONSIDERATION/OTHER 12. The request for reconsideration
has been considered but does NOT place the application in condition for allowance
because:

  With respect to the first argument that includes the affidavit from Dr. James Sheehy
  which the Examiner has reviewed. While the Examiner agrees that the math and physics
  are sound in this application, the Examiner holds that the claims are still not
  enabled. With respect to the second argument on page 4 that "emitting microwaves
  within a copper resonant cavity it is possible to experimentally obtain vibrational
  frequencies in excess of 10 Gigahertz, thus enabling electromagnetic (EM) flux values
  of 10^33 Watts/m^2, according to Equation 1 in the inventor's AIAA paper. This EM flux
  value is equivalent to the generation of E-fields on the order of 10^18 V/m and
  B-fields on the order of 10^9 Tesla. Thus, the fields required f[o]r the invention are
  both feasible and enabled." The Examiner respectfully disagrees. While obtaining high
  vibrational frequencies are possible this does not mean that the E-fields and B-fields
  that are required to polarize the quantum vacuum can be created. If this is so then
  the Examiner asks that the Applicant demonstrate these high vibrational frequencies to
  create a local polarized vacuum.
