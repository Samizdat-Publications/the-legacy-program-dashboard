# The triage rule changes: notability of the claim is a second, parallel criterion

19 September 2026. **Adopted**, on Stewart's instruction, from the proposal drafted as
section 5 of `research/leads/CANON-GAPS-2026-09-18.md` and never acted on.

## 1. What changes, and what does not

**The OLD rule:** a lead becomes a node only when an openable primary record anchors the
claim. That is why this board carries the officials and omits the claimants. An officer's
commission leaves paper; a claimant's account does not.

**The NEW rule adds a second, parallel criterion.** A person, place, programme or event
belongs on the board when **an informed reader would expect to find it and would read its
absence as a gap in the map**, whether or not any record vouches for what was said.

**Nothing about grading changes.** This is the distinction that matters and it is easy to
lose:

| mechanism | what it decides | changed? |
|---|---|---|
| The TRIAGE rule | whether a lead becomes a node at all | **YES, widened** |
| The TIER | how strong the evidence for each claim is | **NO** |

A node admitted on notability is graded exactly as honestly as any other: usually Tier 2
for "X said Y on a dated record", Tier 3 for inference. Its openable anchor is **the dated
artefact of the claim** (a recorded testimony, a broadcast, a book, a filing), not a record
of the thing claimed. The case against goes in the same node's `conflicts` and `unverified`
fields, never in a separate file.

**Where no record of any kind exists, the node says so in its own narrative**, because "no
employment, service or civil record has ever been produced for this person" is itself a
finding, and it is more useful to a reader than silence.

## 2. Why, in Stewart's terms, recorded because the reasoning governs future passes

His position, 19 September: this is the most secret programme since Manhattan. The best
evidence available now is credible trained observers coming forward, F-16 pilots, guards at
nuclear facilities. Applying ordinary scientific proof thresholds guarantees failure,
because every authority's official position is denial, backed by an eighty-year record of
explanations offered in bad faith. The goal is to get all the witness claims on the board,
tagged by tier, and then see where the connections lead.

**The board's own evidence supports the "follow the thread" half of that**, three times in
one night:

- **Fouche's AF Form 77a.** Fifteen pages of self-published certificates that look
  worthless. Page 3 places him on temporary duty at AFFTC Detachment 3, Nellis AF Range,
  and it is the SCEPTIC'S own published reference work that establishes DET 3 is Groom
  Lake.
- **Robert Jacobs.** Got stronger from a hostile source. The project engineer answered him
  by name in *Skeptical Inquirer* and confirmed his unit, his on-site role and his presence
  with team photographs, while disputing what he saw.
- **Thomas Castello.** Visible as a fabrication ONLY because this board tags rather than
  excludes. Had he been dropped as debunked, the clearest documented case of manufactured
  corroboration in the subject would have been lost with him.

## 3. The obligation that comes with the widening, and it is not optional

Admitting notability as a criterion admits the whole circular-reporting problem with it.
This board has now documented that problem from both directions in a single night:

- **Deliberate.** Tal Levesque invented Thomas Castello and mailed a hundred identical
  packets to authors and lecturers, manufacturing the appearance of independent
  corroboration arriving from many directions.
- **Structural, with nobody intending it.** The Disclosure Project Briefing Document's
  sixty-eight testimonies were all collected by one interviewer, who cross-references his
  own witnesses to each other in editorial notes signed "SG".

`CLAUDE.md` section 2 already says two agents agreeing is not corroboration when they share
a method. Under the old rule that was mostly theoretical, because claimants rarely became
nodes. Under the new rule it is load-bearing.

**So every node admitted on notability MUST record the provenance of the claim**, not only
its content:

| field | what it must answer |
|---|---|
| how it reached us | who found this person or this account, and by what route |
| what came first | what, if anything, they said BEFORE being contacted by an interested party |
| what they were given | whether they were presented with another witness's account before speaking |
| where it diverges | agreement on substance with divergence on detail reads very differently from verbatim agreement |

The `michael-herrera` node already does this: its edge to the 2025 hearing carries a note
in capitals saying it is NOT a witness appearance, because a document was moved into the
record by a member and sourced to a third party. That is the standard.

## 4. What this does NOT license

- It does not license a node with no dated artefact at all. There must be something
  openable that fixes WHEN the claim was made, even if nothing supports what it says.
- It does not license upgrading a tier. Testimony is Tier 2. It stays Tier 2 and is carried
  proudly at Tier 2.
- It does not license dropping the case against. It goes on the same node.
- It does not license laundering. "Researchers say" is not a source; name who.

## 5. Consequences to expect

The canon audit's bands 1 and 2 are 80 entries which it estimates at roughly 95 nodes. Many
were blocked by the old rule and are now admissible. Expect the board's Tier 2 and Tier 3
claim counts to grow faster than Tier 1 from here, and expect that to be correct rather
than a degradation: the board is adding the claimant layer it was always missing.

The tier tags are what keep this analysable. They are not a gate on entry, they are the
index that makes the later question askable: show me convergences where at least one leg is
documented, and separately, show me clusters where every leg is Tier 2 and trace whether
they share a source. The second query is how Castello was caught.
