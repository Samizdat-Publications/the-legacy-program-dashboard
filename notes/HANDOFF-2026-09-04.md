## 4. Things a next session must not undo

- **`build_app_data.py`'s fingerprint is content-only.** CRLF-normalised bytes, keyed by forward-slash names, no `.git` read, no `sourceCommit`. Do not put a git id, an mtime or a raw-bytes hash back into anything under `app/public/data/`, and do not put `-I` flags back on the app job's diff.
- **The back button never writes `location.hash`.** `history.back()` only; depth by `replaceState` only. C17's poison exists to catch exactly that regression. `walkAll` and `sweepRoutes` in `run.mjs` include `c17`.
- **`_headers` gives `/data/*` `no-cache`, not `immutable`.** The data files are fetched at fixed paths with no content hash; a year of `immutable` would pin readers to stale research. `/assets/*` is immutable because Vite hashes those names.
- **`_redirects` are 302s** for the old path deep links; `/path/<a>/<b>` links land on the app's not-found page because threads are named chains, not node pairs.
- The old shell keeps building until item 2 above is done deliberately. `deploy.yml` no longer deploys it; nothing else changed.
- Everything in the evening handoff's section 4: the L0 layout arithmetic, the forward-slash source path, the corrections the records forced, the decision list's section E, no em dashes for Stewart, every command block starts with its own quoted `cd`, tables over lists, he cannot read JSON.
