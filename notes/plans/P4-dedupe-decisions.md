## Method

- Signals (same class only; people only against people): label-token Jaccard >= 0.6; id-token Jaccard >= 0.6; a distinctive identifier in one label and the other's label/desc; label Jaccard 0.34-0.6 plus the same full date; label Jaccard 0.25-0.6 plus a URL cited by exactly these two nodes; cross-class label/id Jaccard >= 0.8 (flag only); 2+ shared source URLs (each cited by <= 8 nodes).
- L clusters join on the label-type signals. U clusters are URL-only co-citation pairs whose shared URLs cover at least half of the smaller node's URLs; all were read by label and kept apart. 571 weaker URL-only pairs are listed in candidates.json and not clustered.
- One pair was added by the reviewer, not the script: iaa-fy2024-sec-1102 ~ iaa-fy2024-sec1104 (found by reading the -1102 record).
- Losers' claims and sources are NOT carried by the alias mechanism: merge.py re-points edges, timeline rows and person back-references only. Every loser's claims must be carried to the survivor in research/_corrections.json (as the 2 September collapses did) or they are lost.
