# Phase 4: the topics, for Stewart

Drafted 25 September 2026 (phase 4, task 3), at high effort. **A draft for you to edit line by line.**
Nothing a reader sees uses it until phase 6, which merges only after your look.

**93 topics in 10 sections.** Each topic has a stub in `research/topics/`. The candidate count is how
many board nodes a script proposes for the topic (`tools/topics/propose_members.py`): the anchors, plus nodes
tied to them by an edge (backbone edges for two steps), strengthened by the measured communities, the research
files, the threads and the domain tags. A node needs a real link to an anchor; sharing a community or a research
file alone is not enough. Phase 5 decides the actual members.

Coverage of the proposals: **98.2% of nodes** (1117 of 1138; target 90%) and **97.7% of people** (343 of 351; target 95%) are proposed for at least one topic. Every node with 30 or more links is proposed for three topics or more.

Status: **ok** (as Appendix T had it), **added** (the data showed a group Appendix T missed), **split from** /
**renamed from** / **merged from** (an Appendix T topic reshaped), **research-gap** (an informed reader expects it
and the board cannot yet fill it; it stays in the list so the gap is visible).

Eras on the Atlas: through 1969, 1970-1999, 2000-2016, 2017-2026 (the four the app already uses).
