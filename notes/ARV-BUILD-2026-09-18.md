### What fails and what does not

The check is `no-fit-lands-in-cells`. It has two halves.

**The load-bearing half PASSES.** 8 of 8 fits, at 1366/1600/1920/2560 in both FIT and
FIT ALL, still draw chips. Nobody is landed in the count matrix. That is the regression
Stewart hit on the live deploy twice and it has not returned.

**The corroborating half FAILS.** `fitChipInk` requires `cellInkCells * 2 < cellInkChips`.
Measured: chips 30, cells 16, so 32 < 30 is false. Stable at 30/16 across three runs.
The code's own recorded baseline is "25 per ten-thousand with chips against 9 without".
Chip ink rose a little; **cell-mode ink nearly doubled, 9 to 16**, and that is the change.

### The cause, established rather than guessed

`arv-fluxliner` is the ONLY node in the `witness` band's `program` class. It opens a
band-by-class cell in the count matrix that has never been occupied on this board.
That new cell prints a class-coloured numeral in cell mode, which is exactly what the
measure counts. `northrop-grumman`, the other new L0 node, joins `contractor` x
`institution` which already held five, and contributes nothing.

This is a FIRST-OF-KIND artefact, not a density ceiling. It is the direct structural
consequence of handoff section 3b: a claimed craft is a new kind of object on this
board, so it lands in a band-and-class combination nothing has occupied before.

### The check was not touched, and must not be

It carries a poison and it exists because the regression shipped to Stewart twice.
Weakening it to get a green tick would be the exact failure CLAUDE.md warns about.
