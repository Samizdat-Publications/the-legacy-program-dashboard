# Fit, and the constant that was standing in for something that varies

26 August 2026. Porting Claude Design's shell response (`notes/design-2026-08-26/`).

## What Stewart reported

> "The map is never able to fit ... it really should auto fit until someone clicks
> something so they can see the whole thing." · "Clicking show on the map doesn't do much
> since the map is so obscured by the panels." · "It's also not clear how to collapse the
> right or left panel."

## What was actually wrong, in the order it was found

**1. The opening state.** `mountL0` ended with `if (fk >= L0_LABEL_DROP) centreOn(fk); else
cornerOn(1);`. Below about 1944px of window the map gave up on fitting and opened at 1:1 in
the corner: three of seven bands. Justified once, when below-threshold meant 110 anonymous
rectangles; the dense occupancy mode killed that justification and the default was never
revisited. Now `centreOn(fk)` at every width.

**2. The observer clamped but never re-fitted.**

```js
const ro = new ResizeObserver(() => { measure(); clampView(); repaint(); });
```

Open the dossier, the map narrows, the camera stays exactly where it was and shows less of
the board than a second earlier. This was most of what "the map is obscured" actually meant.
Design's own study hit the same class of bug and named it first in their port order:
*"Every fit must run against a measured rect, never a default. This was the study's own first
bug and it presented exactly as 'the map never fits'."* Ours measured fine and then declined
to act on the measurement.

**3. The head strip's reserve was a constant.** `geo.headRow: 76`, chosen when the control
row was one line of six buttons. Adding the two fit scales and the AUTO/HELD chip made the
row wider than the map at narrow widths, and it overflowed **under the dossier**: the BREATH
button, 15 dead sample points, blocked by `.dock-grip` and `.edoss`. Letting it wrap instead
would have dropped the second line onto the era labels.

Both failures are one mistake, and it is the third time this file has made it — the first two
were `w - 12*k` reserving chip room whether or not a mark was drawn, and the crumb's
`max-width: calc(100% - 24px)`. **A constant standing in for something that varies.** The
strip now reserves what the control row actually occupies, measured after render:

```js
const h = host.querySelector('.l0head').getBoundingClientRect().height;
PAD.t = Math.max(HEAD_MIN, Math.ceil(h) + 20);
```

Any future control can be added without anyone remembering to retune a number, and
`head-row-clear` keeps guarding the result.

## What was ported, and one thing that was deliberately not

| Design | here |
|---|---|
| FIT ROOM / FIT ALL, each printing its scale | **ported, re-derived** — see below |
| auto-fit on entry, released by the first gesture | ported: `AUTO` until the first drag, wheel or pick, then `HELD`. A fit button is the only way back, and its tooltip says so |
| a folded panel keeps its label, one click restores | ported: the dossier folds to a 30px spine reading `DOSSIER — CLICK TO OPEN` |
| chrome in screen space | **already shipped** — `axis-type-readable-at-every-scale` has guarded it since 25 August |

**The one re-derivation.** Design's pair is "fit what is left" against "fit everything and let
the panels cover the overlap". Their study overlays panels on a full-width canvas. This board
does not: the shell is `grid-template-columns: var(--rail-w) minmax(0,1fr) var(--dock-w)`, so
the panels are grid **columns** — they take the map's width rather than covering it. **There
is no overlap here to fit under.** Copying the labels would have shipped a FIT ALL that did
exactly what FIT did and printed a second identical number for ever.

Same information, honest for this layout: FIT fits the room there is; FIT ALL folds the
panels to spines and fits the width that frees. The difference between the two numbers is
still exactly the price of what is open.

## Measured, at a 1920 window

| state | scale | buttons |
|---|---|---|
| on entry | 0.608 | `FIT 0.61×` `FIT ALL 0.62×` **AUTO** |
| dossier open | **re-fits to 0.289** | `FIT 0.29×` `FIT ALL 0.62×` |
| after FIT ALL | **0.62**, host 1890 | panels folded to spines |
| after a wheel | 0.695 | **HELD** |

## And it settles a question geometry could not

`CLAUDE.md` §9 asked whether to reclaim 24px from `geo.gutter` for a labelled fit at 1920.
Tried, and **the note's stated fix is wrong**: `fitScale` is
`(hostW - gutter - 16) / (worldW - gutter)`, so the gutter sits on *both* sides of the ratio
and shrinking it moves numerator and denominator together — 148 → 124 bought 0.608 → 0.613
against a 0.62 threshold. Taking the 24px off `worldW` instead was far worse, 0.379, because
narrower columns force taller cells and the vertical axis starts binding. Both reverted.

**FIT ALL reaches 0.62 at a 1920 window.** The labelled whole-board fit was never a geometry
problem; the map just needed its width back. The question is closed and no geometry changed.
