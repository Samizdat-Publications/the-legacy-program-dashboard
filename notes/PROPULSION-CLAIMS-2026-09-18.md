# The propulsion-mechanism strand, and US 3,322,374 opened

18 September 2026. Stewart passed a patent found in a Reddit thread on "scalar physics",
with the correct caveat that the thread is unreliable. The patent is a real primary
document and was opened. **The thread is wrong about it, and that is the finding.**

Claim 1, in plain terms: coils arranged serially along the craft's axis, driven by polyphase
alternating current, produce a collective magnetic field whose point of maximum flux density
repeatedly travels from the leading end to the trailing end of the craft; that travelling
field induces eddy currents in the surrounding ionised fluid, making the fluid itself an
electrical conductor that is driven backwards, propelling the craft forwards. It is a linear
induction motor using the atmosphere as its armature. Classical electromagnetism.

## 2. The claim made for it is false, and checkably so

The Reddit comment asserts the patent is "ample evidence" that the "Scalar Physics" callout
in section D of Mark McCandlish's ARV drawing refers to spin-zero scalar particles.

Word counts across all 7 pages: **scalar 0, spin 0, gravity 0, anti-gravity 0, zero point 0.**

The single hit for "air force" is the phrase "the ionized air force vector", a false positive.

## 3. Why this is worth keeping: it is the third instance of one pattern

The board already documents, for T. T. Brown, that neither patent claims anti-gravity: both
US 3,187,206 and US 2,949,550 claim thrust by electrical fields in a dielectric medium, and
it is the ACCOUNT that reinterprets this as levitation. The ARV cluster built today carries
that caveat on two edges.

Here the same move happens again: a real, openable, mundane patent is cited as evidence for
a mechanism it never mentions. Brown 1957/58, King 1964, and the ARV account's own
capacitor-stack-as-levitation reading are three worked examples of one failure mode.

**Proposed strand: claimed propulsion mechanisms, graded against the documents cited for
them.** For each mechanism a claim invokes, the strand records what the cited document
actually claims. Candidate mechanisms, from Stewart's own framing on 18 September:

| mechanism | cited documents | status |
|---|---|---|
| Alcubierre-style metric engineering | Alcubierre 1994 and successors | Not opened. Stewart rules it out on his own reasoning: locally bending spacetime above a building would have effects nobody could conceal |
| Magnetohydrodynamics | **US 3,322,374 (King, 1967), opened** | The patent is real and claims exactly MHD. Nothing in it is exotic |
| Electrokinetic / Biefeld-Brown | US 3,187,206, US 2,949,550 | On the board. Neither claims anti-gravity |
| Inertial mass reduction | The Salvatore Pais / US Navy patents | All five patents currently in the board's patents view |
| Hutchison-effect variants | not enumerated | Not opened |

## 4. Recommendation on the patents view: NOT yet

The five patents in the view today are all Pais, and they are there because **the Navy filed
and prosecuted them**. That is the connection to the programme, and it is what makes them
board material rather than interesting reading.

King has no assignee, no contractor, no programme, no clearance and no government interest.
On the board's own test he connects to nothing. Adding him to the patents view because a
patent about MHD turned up in a thread about the ARV would be sourcing by topical
resemblance, which is the thing the board exists not to do.

**He belongs to the strand in section 3, not to the Pais view.** If the strand is built, King
is a strong Tier 1 entry in it: a fully documented 1964 patent for the exact mechanism the
lore invokes, with no secrecy and no sponsor, which is informative precisely because it is
ordinary. Held as a candidate until the strand is decided.
