## 3. Defects found

### #1 · BROKEN · data · node `nip`
Source URL `https://www.odni.gov/index.php/what-we-do/ic-budget` no longer exists. It
silently redirects to `odni.gov/about/`, which contains nothing about Section 601, P.L.
110-53, the NIP, or the 30-day disclosure requirement. The claim may well be true; the
citation does not support it. **Silent redirect, not a 404**, which is why nothing caught it.
Fix: re-source to the statute or an ODNI press release, or demote.

### #2 · BROKEN · data · node `john-greenewald`
Two problems in one claim.
1. The claim cites FOIA case **DODIG-2021-000806**. The source page says
   **DODOIG-2021-000806**. One letter. A reader following the citation to file their own
   request would fail.
2. "Requester of record" is an inference. The page never states Greenewald is the requester.

The rest of the claim (fourth interim release, five pages, ~245 pages pending, 16 June 2026)
is verified exactly.

### #3 · UGLY · data · node `fas`
Claim: FAS brought the **1997** litigation that forced the first disclosure of the total
intelligence budget **in fifty years**. The source says the suit was brought by FAS with Kate
Martin's assistance and that the figure was declassified "for the first time." It does not
say fifty years and does not date the litigation. Two embellishments the source does not
carry. On a board whose one unbendable rule is no invented sources, unsourced intensifiers
are the same failure at lower amplitude.

### #4 · UGLY · data · node `carve-out`
The DoDI 5205.11 glossary quote is cut mid-sentence. Source reads "...relieves DCSA of its
National Industrial Security Program obligation **to perform industrial security oversight
functions for a DoD SAP.**" The claim stops at "obligation," which broadens the definition
into something the directive does not say. Restore the full sentence.

### #5 · Finding · data · node `aawsap-contract-hhm402-08-c-0072`
The claim is a **negative**: the FPDS-NG ATOM feed "returns an empty result feed." The feed
could not be read on re-check; it returned an unrendered XML payload. An unreadable feed and
an empty feed are indistinguishable from outside, which is exactly the `canvasInk` failure
mode in a different costume. This is the board's cleanest piece of evidence that the AAWSAP
contract was kept out of public contract data, so it is worth confirming by hand and
recording *how* it was confirmed.

### #6 · Finding · data · node `sap-taxonomy`
The "waived SAP" definition attributed to DoDD 5205.07 (2010) could not be confirmed. The
Wayback snapshot is unreachable from this session, and the term does not appear in the
current (12 Sep 2024) reissue of 520507p.pdf. The quoted text also reads as two spliced
fragments. Treat as unsupported pending a manual check of the 2010 PDF.

### #7 · Finding · data · node `usc-10-119`
The 10 U.S.C. 119(a)(1) quote is exact, but the statutory notes record that the (a) and (b)
reporting requirements **terminated effective 31 December 2021**. The claim states the
requirement in the present tense. Worth a temporal qualifier, since the board's argument
about SAP reporting depends on whether the duty currently exists.

### #8 · BROKEN · data · board-wide
**90 of 970 nodes have degree 0.** 9.3% of the board has no edge at all and cannot be reached
by traversal from anywhere. This is distinct from, and more severe than, the 346
detail-nodes-with-no-L0-parent problem named in `notes/DESIGN-BRIEF-L0-MAP.md`: those nodes
at least connect to something. These connect to nothing. Any L0 map built before this is
resolved will be built over a hole.

### #9 · BROKEN · tooling · `tools/GOLDEN.json` and `tools/verify.py`
`unique_source_urls: 2678` is not a URL count. `measure()` at `verify.py:197` builds
`{c["source"] ...}`, a set of the full annotated source **strings**. Appending a DEMOTED note
creates a new "unique URL." Actual distinct URLs across claim sources: **154**. The metric
overstates by a factor of 17, not "slightly." Fix: extract URLs with a regex and count those;
keep the string count under an honest name if it is wanted.

### #10 · UGLY · docs · `TESTING-CHECKLIST.md` §0
Asserts ENTITIES 976 / SOURCED LINKS 1788 / 4037 claims. Actual: **970 / 1786 / 4108**. Also
still instructs the reader that the board "runs 1,600 layout steps before first paint," which
`tools/precompute_layout.py` made obsolete at `ac8fce7`. A checklist that fails its own
opening assertion trains the reader to skip it.

## 5. Two limits on this audit, stated plainly

**A. 79% of Tier 1 is not machine-checkable.** 1,845 of 2,345 Tier 1 claims carry no URL.
They cite documents by title, FOIA number and page: "DIA talking paper DR_Reid_Mtg_Nov09, 19
Nov 2009", "Nature 251, 602-607 (1974)". That is legitimate scholarly practice and not
evidence of anything wrong. It does mean the §12 spot-check reaches at most 21% of Tier 1 by
this method, and that a fabricated document number is precisely the defect that would survive
it. Closing that gap needs a human with the documents, or a pass that resolves document
identifiers to a retrievable copy.

**B. `web.archive.org` cannot be fetched from this session.** 162 unique board URLs, roughly
15% of the 1,097 total, are Wayback snapshots. They are unverifiable by the method used here.
Defect #6 is the first consequence.

Neither limit is a reason to distrust the 25 that were checked. Both are reasons not to
report the board as "sources verified" without qualification.
