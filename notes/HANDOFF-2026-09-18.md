## 3b. THE CANON PASS, and the modelling rule behind it (Stewart, 18 September)

McCandlish having no node exposed the hole: **the board was built record-first, and every
corpus pass promoted a lead to a node only when an openable record anchored it. That
filters out exactly the claimants whose story is testimony.** No Bob Lazar, no John Lear,
no Dulce, no Phil Schneider, no Clifford Stone, no Vannevar Bush. One triage file even
says "there is no Bob Lazar node" and nothing acted on it. The material is on disk: Lazar
is in 159 lead files. `research/leads/CANON-GAPS-2026-09-18.md` is the ranked audit.

**Stewart's ruling: a historically notable claimant goes on the board REGARDLESS of tier**,
graded honestly, with the case against on the same node. The openable-record test still
decides a claim's TIER. It no longer decides whether a person gets a NODE.

**And the rule for how they are joined, which matters more than the nodes.** His words,
paraphrased: if ten people make claims about a place, one diagrams it and one says he
worked there, the network map must show that convergence as a set of connections into
that place's node, so a reader sees a node with a lot going on and asks what is happening
there. So:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The thing claimed about is a node, and it is the hub** | A claimed site, craft, event or programme (Dulce, S-4, the ARV, Echo Flight) is a node in its own right even when the only evidence for it is testimony. Its description says plainly that it is claimed, and by whom |
| **Every claimant gets an edge INTO it** | Type `witness-chain` (157 exist already), `claimTier` 2 for a named person on the record, the note in the form "X said, on <occasion>, that ...", the source being the recorded occasion. Never an edge claimant-to-claimant standing in for it: ten people joined to each other and not to Dulce is the failure he described |
| **The edge says what KIND of claimant** | First-hand worked-there, first-hand witness, second-hand relay, illustrator or reconstructor, investigator, debunker. In the note for now; if the map later styles by it, it becomes a field |
| **The case against is an edge too** | Doty and Bennewitz into Dulce as the documented origin of the story is as much a connection as Schneider's. A hub with only believers on it is a false picture. `denial` and `investigative` exist for this |
| **Degree is allowed to mean "a lot is claimed here", never "this is true"** | Size still must not encode importance. What a busy hub shows is where claims converge, and the tier dashes on its edges show how much of that is documented. That is honest, and it is the point |

**The UFO and nuclear weapons thread is the model case and is under-built.** On the board
today: `malmstrom-echo-flight-1967`, `minot-1966`, `minot-1968`, `l-malmstrom`,
`robert-salas`, the `d-nuclear` domain. Missing, as far as a quick look shows: Robert
Hastings and his 2010 National Press Club witnesses, Loring, Wurtsmith, F. E. Warren,
Bentwaters as a weapons site, the Soviet side (the Usovo 1982 incident and the
post-Cold-War document releases), and the US-Soviet agreement that names unidentified
objects. That last one is real and openable and would be Tier 1: the 1971 Agreement on
Measures to Reduce the Risk of Outbreak of Nuclear War, whose Article 3 covers detection
of unidentified objects by missile warning systems (from memory; open it at state.gov
before writing a word of it). The "backline" claim is then graded against what the
treaty text actually says, which is narrower than the lore.

**Phases, per the token rules:** the audit is the map. Then one coordinator agent fans
out Opus verifiers per cluster, each writing a `research/leads/verified-*.md`. Then one
build per cluster or one combined build, after `tier-scope-b` has landed, because two
builds cannot share the working tree.
